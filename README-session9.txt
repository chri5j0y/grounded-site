WHAT'S NEW
  Orientation is the Field level's front door. A Field user sees only Orientation until they finish it.
  Then Home opens, with their status, Print Certificate, and Print Agreement.
    Six lessons: The Grounded way, Presence before tools, Privacy, Safety comes first,
      Scope and referral, Care for the carer and using Grounded well.
    Eight practice scenarios, 7 of 8 to pass. Answers and the why come after. Retake anytime,
      new order each time. Scenarios open after all six lessons are read.
    The Field Guide User Agreement: typed full name plus a checkbox. Opens after passing.
    A printable certificate: name, completed date, renew-by date, certificate number, version.
  Staff and Owner: not locked out of anything. Orientation shows as a card at the top of the
  Training tab, with one extra step: the Staff section (access requests, pricing questions go to
  Chris or Kayti, keeping Staff material private, supporting Field users).
  Renewal: yearly, from the completion date. Renewal opens 30 days early. Field users are gated
  again if it lapses. Lessons stay marked as read; they retake the scenarios and sign again.
  When standards change, the Orientation version goes up and everyone renews the same way,
  with a "What changed" list.
  Staff footer now reads "Soul Tree, The Grove, and Grounded are trademarks of Chris Joy,"
  matching the rest of the site (it still said Tending the Garden).
  Owner tab: the Make a code note no longer says to hold Field codes.

EDITING ORIENTATION LATER
  The lessons, scenarios, and agreement live inside the sealed lib-field.js, so edits go through a
  chat session (send the fresh ZIP). Small wording fixes keep the same version. If a change is
  a new standard people need to know, ask for a version bump plus a "What changed" line.

READ CLOSELY (safety and legal lines)
  Lesson 4 Safety comes first: 911, call or text 988, ask about suicide directly, never share methods
  or pain-based substitutes, abuse and neglect (listen, believe, don't investigate, no secrecy,
  duty to report varies by state and role), Childhelp 1-800-422-4453, "your protocol comes first."
  Lesson 3 Privacy: teen privacy with a safety exception.
  Lesson 5 Scope: no diagnosis, no medication advice unless licensed.
  The agreement is plain language. Have the attorney review it with terms.html and privacy.html.
