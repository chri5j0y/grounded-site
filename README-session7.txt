SAFETY LINES TO READ CLOSELY
  Self-harm: 988, Crisis Text Line (HOME to 741741), 911 for serious injury. No methods described.
    No pain-based "substitutes" (ice, rubber bands), on purpose; they keep the pattern going.
  Eating disorders: no numbers, weights, or calorie talk anywhere. Doctor first.
    ANAD Helpline 1-888-375-7767 (weekdays, 9 to 9 Central). National Alliance for Eating
    Disorders 1-866-662-1235. The Emily Program (Minnesota). 911 for fainting, chest pain, confusion.
  Pornography: non-explicit, values-respecting, shame-free. Sprout flags possible abuse and points
    to "When a child tells you someone hurt them." CyberTipline 1-800-843-5678. Soul Tree names
    Stop It Now for anyone drawn to images of minors.
  Gambling: Minnesota Problem Gambling Helpline 1-800-333-4673 (HOPE), text HOPE to 53342,
    free counseling for families too. Outside Minnesota: 1-800-GAMBLER. Soul Tree names the
    higher suicide risk and 988.
  Car accidents: ER and concussion warning signs (CDC HEADS UP).
  Bedwetting: sends a child who was dry and starts wetting again to the doctor.
  Hotline numbers were checked on the web September 30, 2026.
