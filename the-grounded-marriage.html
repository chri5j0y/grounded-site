Ask About The Grounded Marriage

A wedding is one day. A marriage is every day after. The Grounded Marriage is our premarital program and everything that comes with it: twelve hours of sessions with Chris and Kayti, Heartwood, your private app for the two of you, After the Vows with a first-year check-in, and Your Ceremony, if you would like us to lead your wedding too.

We have been married 22 years, and we lead every session together. The Grounded Marriage is interfaith and built to your faith: your sessions, readings, and practices follow your tradition, both of your traditions, or none. For all faith traditions and everything in-between.

What it is

Everything The Grounded Marriage brings you.

Twelve Hours with Both of Us

Six two-hour premarital sessions with Chris and Kayti, in person or on Zoom: your story and hopes, your Strengths and Growing Edges, speaking and listening, conflict and repair, money, families, and home, then meaning, children, and the road ahead. Honest, practical, and often fun.

Heartwood, Your Private App

The Grounded Marriage comes with Heartwood, your private app for the two of you. It opens with the code from your first session. In Before the Vows, each of you answers the questions privately, then Strengths and Growing Edges and Talk About This show where you stand together. The Couple Workbook, The Money Map, and a video for each session keep you talking between sessions, and Your Tree grows as you do.

Built to Your Faith

Interfaith, and shaped to the two of you: one tradition, two traditions, or none. Each session’s reflections, readings, and practices follow your lead, in Faith or Plain wording.

Minnesota’s Reduced License Fee

Twelve hours of premarital education, with a premarital inventory and communication and conflict skills, qualify you for Minnesota’s reduced marriage license fee. We lead the hours and sign the Educator’s Statement your county requires.

After the Vows

After your wedding, Heartwood opens After the Vows: Practices for Two and the Monthly Check-in for Two. Then comes your First-Year Check-in with both of us: what is growing strong, where you want to grow, and what you hope for the years ahead.

Your Ceremony

Add Your Ceremony, and we write a ceremony for the two of you, built from your story, your vows, and your traditions, with your rehearsal included. Chris leads the day and files your license.

Common questions
Do we have to be religious?

We welcome couples of all faith traditions and everything in-between. The Grounded Marriage is built to your faith, whatever it is, and your sessions follow your lead, in Faith or Plain wording.

What if we come from two traditions?

Many couples do. Heartwood adds questions for a two-tradition home, and we shape a faith session that honors both of you.

How do we get Heartwood?

Heartwood comes with The Grounded Marriage. At your first session we give you a link and a short code, and each of you opens Heartwood on your own phone. Your answers stay on your own devices, encrypted. You can see a sample any time.

Does this count for the reduced license fee?

Minnesota reduces the marriage license fee for couples with at least 12 hours of premarital education that includes a premarital inventory and communication and conflict skills (Minnesota Statutes 517.08). Our 12 hours are built for that: Before the Vows, in Heartwood, is our premarital inventory, and Sessions 3 and 4 teach communication and conflict skills. We sign the Educator’s Statement for your county. Fees can change, so check with your county.

Can we book only part of it?

Yes. The Grounded Marriage, weddings, and rehearsals are each available on their own. The Grounded Marriage + Your Ceremony brings the program, your wedding, and your rehearsal together.

Need a shorter path? Ask us about a 6-hour option.

It covers the heart of the program in three two-hour sessions, with Heartwood, the workbook, and the practices. The reduced license fee needs the full 12 hours, and you can add sessions later to reach them.

Is this therapy?

It is premarital education and support. If you would like clinical support too, we will help you find it.
