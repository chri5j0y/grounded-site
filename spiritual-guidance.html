Spiritual Guidance

One-on-one guidance for your inner life, whatever shape it's in.

Ask About Spiritual Guidance

Sometimes you need someone to think out loud with. Someone who stays steady with doubt, anger, and open questions. These sessions are space to look at what's happening inside you, what you believe, and where you want to go next.

Chris trained in theology and has spent years with people at their most honest. We work with people of all faith traditions and everything in-between, and we always start from where you are.

What it is

What this can look like.

Spiritual Direction

Regular space to notice where meaning, faith, or God is showing up in your life, and where it feels absent.

Faith Questions & Doubt

Room to wrestle honestly and find your own way to an answer. Doubt is welcome here.

Life Transitions & Discernment

Career changes, retirement, loss, new beginnings, and big decisions. Help listening to what you already know.

Healing from Religious Hurt

For people wounded by faith communities who want to find their footing again, whether that leads back to faith or somewhere new.

Coaching for Helpers

For chaplains, clergy, nurses, and caregivers who want to keep doing this work for the long run.

Common questions
How is this different from therapy?

Therapy treats mental health conditions. Spiritual guidance focuses on meaning, faith, values, and the inner life. Many people do both, and we're glad to work alongside a therapist.

Do I have to share your beliefs?

We welcome all faith traditions and everything in-between, and we start from what you believe.

What if I'm in crisis?

If you're in crisis or thinking about ending your life, call or text 988 any time, day or night. We can also help you connect with the right care.
