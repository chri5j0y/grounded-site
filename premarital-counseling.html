Premarital Sessions

The Grounded Marriage: build a strong foundation before the wedding day, with a couple who has been married 22 years.

Ask About The Grounded Marriage

The wedding is one day. The marriage is every day after. Premarital sessions help you understand each other more deeply, talk honestly about the things couples often avoid, and learn skills that carry you through the hard seasons.

Our premarital program is The Grounded Marriage: 12 hours with Chris and Kayti in six two-hour sessions, Heartwood, your private app for the two of you, with Before the Vows, After the Vows, the Couple Workbook, videos, and Practices for Two. It is interfaith and built to your faith.

What to expect

From first hello to the day itself.

  1. 1

    A first conversation

    Meet us, ask questions, and see if it is a fit.

  2. 2

    Before the Vows

    At your first session you get your Heartwood code. Each of you answers the questions on your own, and Strengths and Growing Edges shows where you stand together.

  3. 3

    Six sessions together

    We read your results together and practice communication and conflict skills, two hours at a time, with the Couple Workbook.

  4. 4

    After the Vows

    Practices for Two and a monthly check-in at home, then your First-Year Check-in with us.

Make it yours

Ways we can shape it

  • Communication and resolving conflict
  • Finances and money habits, with The Money Map
  • Family of origin and in-laws
  • Faith and shared values, built to your tradition, two traditions, or none
  • Roles, expectations, closeness, and parenting
  • Your goals and dreams for the years ahead
Good to know

Practical details

  • In Minnesota, 12 hours of premarital education that includes a premarital inventory and communication and conflict skills lowers your marriage license fee to $50 instead of $125. Our 12 hours are built for that, and we sign the Educator's Statement your county requires.
  • Available in person near St. Cloud or on Zoom.
  • Pairs naturally with your wedding ceremony: The Grounded Marriage + Your Ceremony brings both together, with your rehearsal included.

Heartwood, your private app for the two of you.

The Grounded Marriage comes with Heartwood, and it opens with the code from your first session. In Before the Vows, each of you answers privately, then Strengths and Growing Edges and Talk About This show what to talk about next, and where you already agree. After the Vows opens after your wedding. Your answers stay on your own devices.

Common questions
Do we need to be religious?

The Grounded Marriage welcomes couples of all faith traditions and everything in-between, and we build your sessions to your faith, whatever it is.

What if we are already married?

Ask us. After the Vows, in Heartwood, is made for married couples, with Practices for Two and the Monthly Check-in for Two, and we can shape sessions for where you are now.

Is this therapy?

It is premarital education and coaching. If deeper issues come up, we will help you find the right support.
