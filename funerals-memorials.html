Farewells

Funerals and Memorials

Honest, personal services that sound like the person you love.

Plan a Service

When someone dies, the service is one of the last things you get to do for them. It should sound like them: their humor, their faith or lack of one, their stubbornness, the way they loved.

Chris has stood at bedsides and gravesides for years as a chaplain. We meet with your family, gather stories, and write a service and eulogy that honor a real life, and we coordinate with the funeral home so you have one less thing to manage.

What to expect

From first hello to the day itself.

  1. 1

    A first conversation

    Call or write, even in the first hours. We will listen and explain the next steps.

  2. 2

    Gathering stories

    We meet with family, in person or on Zoom, to hear who they were.

  3. 3

    Writing the service

    We draft the service and eulogy and send it for your family's notes.

  4. 4

    The day

    We arrive early, coordinate with the funeral home, and lead with care so you can grieve.

Make it yours

Ways we can shape it

  • A eulogy built from your family's stories
  • Readings, scripture, poems, or songs they loved
  • Moments for family and friends to share
  • Rituals like candle lighting or a memory table
  • Graveside committal or a scattering of ashes
Good to know

Practical details

  • We coordinate directly with your funeral director.
  • Services can be at a funeral home, church, graveside, home, or outdoors.
  • If a death is close, put URGENT in your message and we will respond as fast as we can.
For your family

Tools for Families

Gentle helps for the days around a death, to use on your own or with us. Your words stay on your device.

  • Planning a Farewell: a gentle checklist for the first hours, the first days, the service, and after.
  • Obituary Helper: a Death Notice, a Newspaper Obituary, and an Online Obituary in your own words.
  • Eulogy Helper: prompts, a simple outline, and a reading time for the one who will speak.
Common questions
Do you work with families who are not religious?

Yes. Many of our services are for families of no particular faith.

Can you lead a service on short notice?

We will do everything we can to be there.

Do you help write the obituary?

We are glad to help you shape it. You can also start a draft on your own with the Obituary Helper.

How soon can you come?

When a death has just happened or is close, mark your message urgent, and we'll usually reach out the same day. We gladly work alongside your funeral home and hospice team.
