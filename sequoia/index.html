A long life holds a great deal: work and love, losses and recoveries, and a faith or a way of seeing the world carried for decades. Later life brings its own changes too. Retirement, health, moves, and goodbyes can shake parts of a life that held steady for years.

Resilience is your ability to bounce back when something unexpected and unwanted happens. It keeps growing at every age. The key is tending the different parts of your life, so when one part is hurt, the healthy parts can hold you while it heals.

A person is a lot like a tree. Sequoias are among the oldest living things on earth, and they keep growing all their lives. Sequoia looks at all six parts of you, from root to fruit.

One Rule

Name each resource in only one part of your tree. If your family gives you hope, they still belong in the Branches, not the Fruit. This shows your true resources in each part, and where you may be leaning on one part of the tree to carry weight it was never meant to hold alone.
