Readings Library

Words to read aloud, for all faith traditions and everything in-between.

Prayers, scripture, blessings, poems, hymns, songs, and stories, gathered from every faith tradition, from the churches and schools within them, and from the paths outside organized religion. Choose words for a bedside, a funeral, a wedding, a new baby, a blessed home, or an ordinary morning. Read slowly. Many readings have Listen, so you can hear them read aloud.
