Obituary Helper

Answer a few questions, and get three drafts to shape in your own words.

Writing about someone you love, in the middle of missing them, is hard work. Answer what you can, skip the rest, and the helper puts your words into three drafts: a short Death Notice, a Newspaper Obituary, and a longer Online Obituary. Every word stays yours to change.

Your answers and drafts stay on this device, private to you. Clear Everything at the bottom removes them.
