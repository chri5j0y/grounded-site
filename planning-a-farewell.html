Tools for Families

Planning a Farewell

A gentle checklist for the hours, days, and weeks around a death.

When someone you love is dying, or has just died, there is a lot to do and very little room to think. This list holds it for you. Any order works, and most of it can wait. Check things off as they’re done, skip what doesn’t fit your family, and hand any of it to someone who has asked how they can help.

Your check marks stay on this device, private to you. Clear My Checks at the bottom removes them.

If someone is in danger, or a death was sudden or unexpected, call 911.

If hospice is caring for them and the death was expected, call the hospice’s 24/7 line first, not 911. The hospice nurse comes to confirm the death and helps with the next steps.

In crisis or thinking about ending your life? Call or text 988, any time.

Send to Your Officiant

Starting at home? Answer what you can, skip the rest, and send it to the person who will lead the service, so you can pick up together right where you left off. Your answers stay on this device until you choose to send them.
