Welcome

Grounded stands for living alcohol-free and free of harmful drugs: a clear mind, a healthy body, and a life you're fully present for.

Wherever you are, you belong here. Thinking about a change, counting your first days, many years along, or loving someone who drinks or uses: we meet you right where you are, and we walk with you toward an alcohol-free life, one day at a time.

Chris, one of Grounded's founders, has been sober since 2008. Friends see that life and ask how he does it. Living Sober is our answer, shared with anyone who wants it: encouragement for today, short videos, ninety days for the early days, and a private place to grow in Birch, Oak, and Sequoia.

Five Stages, Your Tree

Wherever you are on the path.

In Birch, Oak, and Sequoia you choose your stage privately, and Living Sober fits itself to you: the guides, the videos, and the growth plan ideas for where you are.

  1. 1

    Thinking About a Change

    Honest questions, a gentle look at what drinking or using does for you and costs you, and safe first steps, starting with a doctor.

    For anyone curious about drinking or using less, or stopping.

  2. 2

    Early Days

    The first 90 days: sleep, cravings, mornings, evenings, and people in your corner. One day at a time, with Early Days: 90 Days alongside.

    For the first weeks and months.

  3. 3

    Growing Sober

    Healing what you drank over, repairing relationships, sober friendships and celebrations, and a life that keeps getting fuller.

    For the first years.

  4. 4

    Long-Term Sober

    Still growing: new seasons, staying honest, helping others, and marking your anniversaries with gratitude.

    For years along the path.

  5. 5

    Someone I Love

    Steady support for loving a parent, partner, child, or friend who drinks or uses, and for tending your own tree too.

    For family and friends.

  6. Your Stage Stays Yours

    Your stage, your sober date, and your plan stay on your device, locked in your own profile. Only you see them.

    Private by design.

My Anchor Plan

A plan for hard moments, made on a good day.

Hard moments can come at any hour. My Anchor Plan is ready before they do, in your own words:

  • Who I'll call or text, with their numbers
  • What I'll do, from a short list of ideas or your own
  • Where I'll go
  • What I'll remind myself, a mantra that steadies you
  • The help lines, close at hand

Make yours in your tree. It stays locked in your profile, ready on your phone whenever you need it.

Help Lines

Every kind of help is welcome.

A doctor is a strong first call, especially for anyone who drinks or uses every day, because stopping all at once can need medical support. Meetings, treatment, medicine, therapy, faith, and friends all help people grow sober.

  • SAMHSA National Helpline1-800-662-4357, any time, day or night, in English and Spanish. Treatment referral and information.
  • FindTreatment.govA national directory of treatment by place. findtreatment.gov
  • SMART RecoveryPeer groups for growing sober, and Family and Friends meetings, in person and online. smartrecovery.org
  • NACoAHelp for kids whose family member drinks or uses, and for the grown-ups who love them. nacoa.org
  • Poison Control1-800-222-1222, any time, for questions about a medicine, a drug, or a possible poisoning.

Support any time: 988, call or text · 911, emergency. If someone is hard to wake or breathing slowly, call 911.
