The 4-Week Classroom Pilot

Four weeks. About ten minutes a day.

A week by week plan for one classroom, a kickoff video and start sheet, a weekly check-in with the Grounded team, and a one page summary for your principal.

Week by Week

Your four weeks.

Before You Begin

Kickoff Day

  • Watch the kickoff video (two minutes) and print the start sheet.
  • Choose your times. Most classes use the morning, right after recess, before a test, and the end of the day.
  • Open The Grove on the classroom computer, choose Start Our Grove, then Classroom, and Public School for Plain wording.
  • Tell your class that for four weeks you will try short calm and connection practices together.
Week 1

Settle In

  • One Class Reset a day, right after recess: Calm Countdown on Monday, Balloon Breath on Tuesday, Shake It Out on Wednesday, Calm Corner Breath on Thursday, Goodbye Circle on Friday.
  • About five minutes a day. Press play and do it with your class.
  • Friday: the two minute weekly check-in about what you noticed.
Week 2

Build the Habit

  • Teacher's Day: add a Morning Circle (Warm Hello or One Word Check-in) to your After Recess Reset.
  • Before a test or a big lesson: a Movement Break or Tree and Mountain Poses.
  • About ten minutes a day.
  • Friday: the weekly check-in.
Week 3

Grow Together

  • The practice cards on the Class Screen: a Talking Piece Round, a Kindness Round, and Our Class Promise.
  • Set up a Calm Corner with the Calm Corner card, and show the class how to use it.
  • Keep the daily resets your class likes best.
  • Friday: the weekly check-in.
Week 4

Look Back

  • Keep the routine and add Proud Moments Share or Three Good Things.
  • Look How Far We've Come: remember together where you started.
  • Friday: the last weekly check-in, then print the pilot summary for your principal.
  • A short conversation with the Grounded team about what comes next for your class or your school.

With You Each Week

The Grounded team checks in.

Each week, the Grounded team reaches out with a short note: what is working, what to try next, and any question you have. Your weekly check-in is about what you notice in your room as the teacher. It asks students nothing and keeps no student names.
