Questions from Teachers and Schools

How much time does it take?

About ten minutes a day, split across the day. Most Class Resets take one to three minutes.

What do I need?

A classroom computer, smartboard, or projector with sound. The Grove opens in the browser. Nothing to install.

What ages is it for?

Kindergarten through middle school, and the Class Screen works well for high school advisory and small groups too.

Is it right for a public school?

Yes. A public school class grove uses Plain wording about class life, and every practice welcomes all faith traditions and everything in-between. A faith-based school can choose Faith wording.

What happens to student information?

Students simply join in, and their names stay in the room. Students have no accounts, and the class grove stays on the classroom device.

Who are the voices in the videos?

Calm recorded voices made for Grounded, the same voice from start to finish in each video.

Can our whole school join?

Yes. Whole School pricing covers every classroom in the building, and districts with three or more schools take 20% off each school.
