The Grove for Classrooms

Calm, connected classrooms in a few minutes a day. Short guided Class Resets, a Class Screen for the smartboard, and painted practices a whole class can follow, with no prep for teachers.

What Is Inside

  • 12 Class Resets: one to three minute guided videos with a calm recorded voice.
  • The Class Screen: any practice up big, one step at a time, with a timer.
  • Teacher's Day: picks for the morning, after recess, before a test, and the end of the day.
  • 98 painted practice cards for calm, kindness, movement, gratitude, and ending well.
  • When Life Changes guides for classes, like Big Feelings in the Classroom.

Three Steps

  1. A 4-Week Pilot in one classroom ($49, credited toward the year).
  2. About ten minutes a day at the times that fit the class.
  3. A one page summary of what the teacher noticed.

Private by Design

  • The class grove stays on the classroom device.
  • Students simply join in: no student accounts, and their names stay in the room.
  • Plain wording for public schools, welcoming all faith traditions and everything in-between.
