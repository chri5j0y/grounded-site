Our Four Weeks

  1. Settle In. One Class Reset a day, after recess.
  2. Build the Habit. Add a Morning Circle.
  3. Grow Together. Practice cards, a Calm Corner, and our class promise.
  4. Look Back. Notice how far we have come.

Each Friday

Two minutes for the weekly check-in, about what I notice in our room.

The 12 Class Resets

  • Calm Corner Breath
  • Balloon Breath
  • Calm Countdown
  • Shake It Out
  • Movement Break
  • Tree and Mountain Poses
  • Name It to Tame It
  • One Word Check-in
  • Talking Piece Round
  • Kindness Round
  • Warm Hello
  • Goodbye Circle

How to Play One

  1. Open The Grove on the classroom computer and choose our class grove.
  2. Tap a Class Reset, or a card's Class Screen, and choose Full Screen.
  3. Press play and do it with the class. A calm voice leads it.

The Grove for Classrooms, by Grow With Grounded, St. Cloud, Minnesota. Everything stays on the classroom device, and students simply join in.
