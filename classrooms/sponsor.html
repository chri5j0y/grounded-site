Questions about Sponsoring

What does a sponsored class receive?

A full year of The Grove for Classrooms: 12 Class Resets, the Class Screen, Teacher's Day, 98 painted practice cards, and When Life Changes guides for classes, with the Grounded team helping the teacher begin.

Can I choose the class?

Yes. Name a teacher or a school, or ask the Grounded team to match you with a class that asked for a sponsor.

Will my name be shared?

Only if you choose. The class can thank you by name, or as a friend of the school.

Is it right for any school?

Yes. Public schools use Plain wording about class life, welcoming all faith traditions and everything in-between.
