For Districts

District Overview

The Grove for Classrooms brings calm, connected classrooms to every school in a district, a few minutes a day, with one invoice and one point of contact.

How a District Starts

  1. One school's pilot. A 4-Week Classroom Pilot in one or more classrooms ($49 each, credited toward the year).
  2. A look at the results. Each teacher's one page summary of what they noticed.
  3. The schools that are ready. Whole School plans for each building, with the district price.

School Prices, a Year

School sizePriceWith the district price
Up to 10 classrooms$1,250$1,000
11 to 25 classrooms$1,950$1,560
26 or more classrooms$2,750$2,200

Districts with three or more schools take 20% off each school's price. For example, five medium schools come to $7,800 a year.

What Every School Receives

  • 12 Class Resets and the Class Screen
  • Teacher's Day picks
  • 98 painted practice cards
  • When Life Changes guides for classes
  • A kickoff for staff

Sponsors and Grants

  • Local businesses, churches, and families can sponsor a classroom ($199) or a whole school.
  • The Grounded team can help match a school with a sponsor.
  • Grant-ready words, costs, and outcomes are ready for a school's own application.

Private by Design

Each class grove stays on the classroom device. Students simply join in, with no student accounts, and public schools use Plain wording that welcomes every family.
