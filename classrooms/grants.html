What It Is

The Grove for Classrooms, by Grow With Grounded, gives each classroom short guided Class Resets (one to three minute videos with a calm recorded voice), a Class Screen that puts any practice up big for the whole room, Teacher's Day picks for the morning, after recess, before a test, and the end of the day, and 98 painted practice cards for calm, connection, movement, kindness, and gratitude. It takes about ten minutes a day and needs no prep from the teacher.

Need and Purpose

Teachers need simple, ready tools to help a class settle, focus, and treat each other with care, especially after recess, before tests, and through hard weeks. The Grove for Classrooms supports a calm, connected classroom culture in a few minutes a day, so more of the day goes to learning.

Activities

Week 1: one Class Reset a day after recess. Week 2: a Morning Circle and an After Recess Reset each day. Week 3: practice cards on the Class Screen, a class Calm Corner, and a class promise. Week 4: reflection practices and a summary of what the teacher noticed. After the pilot, the class continues the daily routine all year.

Outcomes the Teacher Notices

Each Friday the teacher records what they notice, in their own words: how quickly the room settles after a reset, calm moments, kindness between students, teaching time back after transitions, and how the teacher feels at the end of the day. The pilot asks students nothing and keeps no student names. At the end, the teacher prints a one page summary for the principal.

Privacy

The class grove stays on the classroom device. Students simply join in, with no student accounts, and their names stay in the room. Public schools use Plain wording about class life that welcomes every family.

Budget

4-Week Classroom Pilot: $49 per classroom, credited toward the year. The Grove for Classrooms: $199 per classroom per year. Whole School: $1,250 a year up to 10 classrooms, $1,950 for 11 to 25, $2,750 for 26 or more. Districts of three or more schools: 20% off each school's price.

About Grow With Grounded

Grow With Grounded, based in St. Cloud, Minnesota, makes research-informed tools for whole person wellbeing for every age. The Grounded team includes a chaplain with years in intensive care, behavioral health, and hospice, and a certified yoga, breathwork, and meditation instructor.

Where Schools Look

Funding that often fits.

  • Title IV, Part A (Student Support and Academic Enrichment). Federal money your district already receives, with a "safe and healthy students" area. Your district's federal programs coordinator decides how it is used.
  • Local community foundations. Many fund K to 12 programs for student wellbeing; in central Minnesota, the Central Minnesota Community Foundation.
  • Grants for teachers. For example, NEA Foundation Student Success Grants for public school educators who are NEA members.
  • Sponsors. A business, church, or family can Sponsor a Classroom, and we can help match your school.

Each funder sets its own rules and dates; check with them before you apply. Need a letter or a quote for your application? Ask the Grounded team.
