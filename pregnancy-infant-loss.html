Farewells

Pregnancy and Infant Loss

Gentle support and ceremony for parents and families after miscarriage, stillbirth, or the death of a baby.

Reach Out for Support

Losing a baby is a grief the world often struggles to hold. Your love for your child is real, and so is your loss, whether you had weeks, months, or only moments together.

Chris is trained through Resolve Through Sharing to support parents, families, and caregivers through pregnancy and infant loss, and he has served as a pediatric and NICU chaplain. We come gently, go at your pace, and help you honor your baby in the ways that feel right to you.

From first hello to the day itself.

Common questions
Is it too late to hold a memorial?

Any time is the right time. Many families mark a loss months or years later.

Do you support partners and grandparents?

Yes. Everyone who loved your baby is welcome.

How soon can you come?

When a death has just happened or is close, mark your message urgent, and we'll usually reach out the same day. We gladly work alongside your funeral home and hospice team.
