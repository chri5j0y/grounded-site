The Grounded Way: Built to Thrive

Whole person health for mind, body, spirit, and relationships, and a first step everyone takes home.

Book This Talk

Healthy living grows from small things tended every day. This talk gives your people a simple way to begin, and the app to keep going.

What the Room Receives

A whole, healthy life, made simple.

Four Parts to Tend

Roots for spirit, Bark for mind, Leaves for body, and Branches for relationships: a simple map of a whole, healthy life.

Real Stories

Stories from years of chaplaincy, told with warmth, that show what tending looks like in a real life.

Practices for Tomorrow

Whole food, water, movement, rest, time outdoors, and unplugged time together, in small steps anyone can take.

The Grounded Way App

Everyone leaves with The Grounded Way app and a first step for this week, kept private on their own device.

Everyone wants to feel strong, clear, and close to the people they love. We help them begin.

Chris Joy is a chaplain with years in intensive care, behavioral health, and hospice, a master's degree in theology, and a weekly writer of real stories. He has lived sober since 2008. Kayti Joy is a certified yoga, breathwork, and meditation instructor who has taught in classrooms and studios.

The Story

Tend four, grow two.

A room settles in after a long week. They meet a tree with four parts to tend, take one slow breath together, and open The Grounded Way on their phones. Each person picks one first step: a walk after dinner, water before coffee, an evening unplugged with the people they love. Small things, tended every day, and meaning and hope begin to grow.

Common questions

Good to know.

What does everyone take home?

The Grounded Way app, kept private on each person's own device, a take-home handout, and one first step for the week.

Is it for every age?

Yes. The Grounded Way has a tree for every age, from Maple for kids to Sequoia for older adults, and the talk is shaped to the people in your room.

Can you shape it for our people?

Yes. Every talk and training starts with a prep call about your people and what they are carrying right now.

Is it faith based?

Each talk welcomes all faith traditions and everything in-between. A church may ask for faith woven in, and a school or workplace may ask for plain words. Both are welcome.
