Meditation, Sound & Movement

A quiet place for your body to settle and breathe again.

Ask About a Session

When life gets hard, the body keeps carrying it long after the mind is tired of thinking about it. Gentle practices like breathing, stillness, sound, and movement help a nervous system on high alert settle down.

Chris led daily meditations on a behavioral health unit, with crystal sound bowls, body scans, and mindfulness groups. Kayti is a certified yoga, breathwork, and meditation instructor who has led weekly sessions for years. Together they create a calm, welcoming space where every experience level is welcome.

What it is

What this can look like.

Sound Bowl Meditation

Guided rest with crystal sound bowls. Just lie back or sit comfortably and let the tones carry you. Many people find it the first time in weeks their shoulders drop.

Body Scan and Mindfulness

A slow, guided walk through the body, noticing with kindness. Simple mindfulness practices you can take home and use on hard days.

Yoga and Breathwork

Gentle yoga and breathing practices led by Kayti, adapted for every body and every age, from chairs to mats.

Groups for Care Teams

Sessions for hospice teams, nurses, caregivers, schools, and grief groups, built to help the people who care for everyone else recover and reset.

Common questions
Do I need any experience?

Every session is beginner-friendly, and you can simply rest and listen.

Is this religious?

These practices welcome all faith traditions and everything in-between. If you want your own tradition woven in, we are glad to include it.

What if I have physical limits?

Everything is adaptable. You can sit, lie down, or stay in a chair, and every movement is optional.

Can you come to our workplace?

Yes. We bring sessions to hospice teams, hospitals, schools, churches, and community groups.
