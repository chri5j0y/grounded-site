Tools

Tools for life's thresholds.

A tree for every age and stage.

Growth tools built from real chaplain work, for every age and stage. Each tree helps people notice and tend the six parts that make them whole: Roots, Trunk, Bark, Branches, Leaves, and Fruit. So when life gets hard, there is something to hold onto. Your answers stay on your device. They are not therapy or medical care.

The Trees

A tree for every age and stage, each with its own check-in, daily practices, and growth plan.

From Maple to Sequoia, every tree shows what is Strong, Steady, and a Growing Edge, offers daily tending, When Life Changes guides with two short videos each, and Learn videos, and comes with Backup, Read Aloud, and Save or Print. A Health and Ability box in your profile opens guides and practices for living with a disability, illness, or difference, and a private Living Sober box opens support for growing sober and for loving someone who drinks or uses.

Maple

Bright leaves, strong roots.

A gentle check-in for kids in grades K to 5, made to do together at home or at school. Kids grow their own tree, practice a little each day, and watch critters come to visit. Grown-ups get When Life Changes: 73 guides for hard talks, from a pet dying to scary news, each with two short videos, one for the child and one for the grown-up. Maple has short Learn videos too.

Aspen

Rooted together.

A check-in for grades 6 to 8, with separate questions for each grade, done on their own or with a grown-up. Every check-in adds a growth ring and leaves to their tree, and daily tending happens right in Aspen. Their tree also stands in The Grove with the family's. Grown-ups get When Life Changes: 64 guides for hard talks with middle schoolers, from group chats and AI chatbots to grief, each with two short videos, one for the student and one for the grown-up. Aspen has short Learn videos too.

Pine

Stand tall through every season.

A check-in for high schoolers, grades 9 to 12, for the years students learn to stand on their own. Six parts of one tree, with questions written for grades 9 to 10 and 11 to 12, a growth plan with practices, and a tree that grows stronger with every day tended. Next Steps is a private notebook for goals, values, and plans after high school. When Life Changes offers 65 guides for the changes of high school, each with two short videos, and Pine has short Learn videos too. Answers open only with the teen's own passcode, and the grown-up who agreed sees only a quiet reminder to check in.

Birch

New ground, deep roots.

A check-in for young adults, 18 to 26, for the years of new ground: school or training, work, service, parenting, or something in between. Six parts of one tree, a growth plan with practices, and a tree that grows stronger with every day tended. Groundwork is a private notebook for who you're becoming, your plans, and the skills you already have. When Life Changes offers 65 guides for the changes of these years, each with two short videos, and Birch has short Learn videos too. Answers open only with your own passcode. Helpers are optional: only you can turn them on, and they see only what you choose to share.

Sequoia

A long life, still growing.

A check-in for older adults, built for 60 and up, for the years of retirement, change, and deep roots. Six parts of one tree, one question at a time in larger text, a growth plan with practices that work seated, standing, or in bed, and a Legacy Book for the stories and lessons you want to pass on. Sequoia has short Learn videos too, and When Life Changes offers 59 guides for later life, from retirement and moving to grief and memory changes, each with a video for you and one for the person helping you. A family member can help with their own passcode, only if you turn that on, and helpers get a check-in of their own.

Willow

Held gently, all the way home.

A tree for the last part of the path, built for two: the person in hospice, and the people who love them. Gentle check-ins that always ask about faith first and record who answered, a page for what matters most, Cuttings for the stories and letters they want to leave, a log of what helped today, and faith cards for 29 traditions. When Life Changes offers 22 guides for the end of life, each with two short videos, one for the person and one for the helper, and Willow has short Learn videos too. Helpers open it with their own passcode and see only what the person chooses to share.

The Grove

Where our trees grow together.

Shared ground for a family, a faith community, a small group, or a team. Check in together with one shared answer for each question, make a Growth Plan, and do small practices side by side. When Life Changes Together offers 69 guides for the changes you go through as a group, from a new baby or a move to a loss, a leader leaving, or a hard season at work, each with a video for the group and one for the leader, and Do This Together videos lead small practices you share. In a family, everyone's own tree from Maple, Aspen, Pine, Birch, Oak, or Sequoia stands side by side. Your tree is yours. The grove is ours.

For Couples

For couples in The Grounded Marriage, before the wedding and after. Your answers stay on your own devices.

Heartwood

Your private app for the two of you, with The Grounded Marriage.

Heartwood comes with The Grounded Marriage and opens with the code from your first session. In Before the Vows, each of you answers privately across twelve areas of married life: money, families, faith and meaning, conflict and repair, and more. Then Strengths and Growing Edges and Talk About This show where you stand together, with a question to start each conversation, plus the Couple Workbook and The Money Map. After the Vows opens after your wedding, with Practices for Two and the Monthly Check-in for Two. Each of you on your own phone.
