Common questions

Before you reach out.

Do I need to be religious to work with you?

We work with people of all faith traditions and everything in-between. Prayer, scripture, and ritual are offered whenever they're wanted, always at your lead.

Where do you work?

We're based in St. Cloud, Minnesota. Travel is included for the first 30 miles, and beyond that it is charged at the IRS mileage rate. Planning can happen on Zoom, and ceremonies can be live streamed for loved ones far away.

Will you travel for our wedding?

Yes, for a few weddings each year. We prepare with you online, and you cover flights and lodging, plus $250 for each travel day.

What comes with a wedding?

A first conversation, getting to know your story, a custom ceremony, the rehearsal if you want one, leading the day, signing and returning your license, and Heartwood, the couples app, for your first year together.

What does it cost?

Every ceremony lists its rate on this page, and all of them are on our Rates and Accessibility page.

What if money is tight?

Everyone is welcome, whatever your budget. Tell us what's possible, and we'll find a rate that works. A few ceremonies each month are set aside as a gift or at a reduced rate for families in hardship.

How quickly will you respond?

We reply to every message within two days, usually sooner. If a death has happened or is close, check the urgent box, and we'll usually reach out the same day.
