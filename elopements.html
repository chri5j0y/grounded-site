Elopements

Just the two of you, or a handful of people you love, anywhere that matters to you.

Plan Your Elopement

An elopement is a wedding at its simplest: the two of you, at your own pace, and the promise itself. It can happen on a lakeshore at sunrise, in a backyard, a park, a courthouse lawn, or a favorite trail.

Small is every bit as meaningful. We still write your ceremony around your story, and we make sure everything legal is handled so you can simply be present.

What to expect

From first hello to the day itself.

  1. 1

    A first conversation

    Tell us your place, your date, and who, if anyone, will be there.

  2. 2

    Your story

    A shorter conversation to hear your story and what you want to say to each other.

  3. 3

    A ceremony written for you

    A short, heartfelt ceremony, with vows written or spoken from the heart.

  4. 4

    The moment

    We meet you there, lead the ceremony, and sign and return your license. Two adult witnesses are needed, and we can help you find them.

  5. 5

    Heartwood: After the Vows

    Every couple receives Heartwood: After the Vows, a private app for your first year together.

Make it yours

Ways we can shape it

  • A ceremony of 5 to 15 minutes, as simple or rich as you want
  • Outdoor and unusual locations welcome
  • Personal vows, or repeat-after-me vows
  • A photographer, a picnic, or just the two of you
  • A quiet blessing or prayer if you want one
Good to know

Practical details

  • In Minnesota, two adult witnesses must be present. If you want it to be just the two of you, ask us about options.
  • Get your license at any county office. It is valid anywhere in Minnesota for six months.
  • Elopements under 20 guests have a reduced rate.
  • Weather plans matter for outdoor spots. We will help you think it through.
Common questions
How small can it be?

Just the two of you and two witnesses is perfectly legal.

Can we elope on short notice?

Often, yes. Reach out and we will do our best.

Will it still feel personal?

Yes. Every elopement is written for you.

How far will you travel?

Anywhere. Travel is included for the first 30 miles. Beyond that, travel is charged at the IRS mileage rate, plus airfare or lodging when needed. We'll marry you in a barn, a courthouse, by a lake, or in your living room.

What if we come from two traditions, or somewhere in-between?

We write ceremonies for interfaith couples, couples from two traditions, and couples who want a fully secular ceremony. For example: "You come from different places and different stories. Today you choose one road, walked side by side."
