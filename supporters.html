Supporters

Thank You, Supporters

You help these stories reach the people who need them.

Every story here is a real story from the edge of life. Your support keeps them coming, one each Wednesday, and we are so grateful you are here.

Supporters are paid subscribers on Substack and friends who support Grow With Grounded in other ways. Each Supporter receives a personal code that opens everything below.

What You Receive

Every Grounded Story in Full

Watch each story narrated, start to finish, or read it at your own pace. Every story, every time.

Early Previews

New stories reach you first, often before they arrive on Substack.

A Code Just for You

One personal code, yours to keep. It opens Grounded Stories on each device you use.

The Story Behind the Story

Chris's own notes on how a story came to be, after the story, read aloud, on the stories where he adds one.

The Monthly Letter

A letter each month from Grow With Grounded, read aloud.

Reflection Cards

A printable card each month: a line from a Grounded Story and a practice to carry it into your week.

How to Use Your Code

  1. Open Grounded StoriesOn your phone, tablet, or computer, go to growwithgrounded.com/stories.
  2. Enter Your CodeTap Enter Your Code and type it just as you received it: three words and a number, like acorn-meadow-lantern-42.
  3. Enjoy Every StoryEvery story opens in full and stays open on that device. You enter your code once on each device.

Your code stays on your device. Grounded Stories keeps it right in that browser, so the stories open each time you come back.

Sharing a device, or passing it along? Tap Forget This Device on Grounded Stories, and that device returns to previews. Your code works again whenever you like.

A New Phone or Computer

Enter Your Code Once More

Open Grounded Stories on the new device and enter your code. Every story opens there too.

Keep Your Code Handy

Save it somewhere safe, like your notes or your password keeper. If you would like it sent again, write to us at hello [at] growwithgrounded [dot] com.

Talk with Us on Substack

Many stories carry a link to Talk about this on Substack, where comments and conversation live. We love hearing what a story stirred in you.

Founders, Staff, and Guides of Grow With Grounded receive everything Supporters do. Their Field Guide code opens Grounded Stories as well.
