Weddings

Your story, your vows, and a ceremony that sounds like the two of you.

Plan Your Wedding

A wedding ceremony is the heart of the whole day. It is the part your guests will remember, and the part you will want to remember too. We write every ceremony from scratch, built around your story, your people, and the promises you want to make.

Whether you want something traditional, something playful, something deeply spiritual, or fully secular, we will shape it with you. Your officiant leads with a calm, warm presence, so you can simply be there.

What to expect

From first hello to the day itself.

  1. 1

    A first conversation

    Tell us about the two of you, your date, your place, and the feeling you want.

  2. 2

    Getting to know your story

    We meet in person or on Zoom and ask the questions that bring out your story: how you met, what you love about each other, and what you hope for.

  3. 3

    Writing your ceremony

    We draft a custom ceremony and send it for your notes. We can help you write your vows too, and we revise until it feels right.

  4. 4

    Rehearsal and the day

    We run the rehearsal if you want one, arrive early on the day, lead the ceremony, and sign and return your marriage license.

  5. 5

    Heartwood: After the Vows

    Every couple receives Heartwood: After the Vows, a private app for your first year together.

Make it yours

Ways we can shape it

  • Vows written by you, with as much or as little help as you want
  • Readings, prayers, or poems from your tradition, or words of your own
  • Rituals like a unity candle, sand ceremony, handfasting, or love letter box
  • Ways to include children, parents, and blended families
  • A moment to remember loved ones who have died
  • Your own words, humor, and personality throughout
Good to know

Practical details

  • In Minnesota, you get your marriage license at any county office, and it is good anywhere in the state for six months. Bring two adult witnesses to the ceremony.
  • Travel is included for the first 30 miles. Beyond that, travel is charged at the IRS mileage rate.
  • Travel weddings: we prepare with you online, and you cover flights and lodging, plus $250 for each travel day. We say yes to a few each year.
  • Planning ahead helps, but we also do weddings on short notice when we can.

Heartwood, for the two of you.

Every couple we marry receives Heartwood, your private app for the two of you, as part of the wedding. Planning a wedding is full of decisions, and Before the Vows helps with the bigger ones: each of you answers privately, then Strengths and Growing Edges and Talk About This show what to talk about together. Your answers stay on your own devices.

Common questions
Do we have to be religious?

We lead ceremonies for people of all faith traditions and everything in-between. Your ceremony reflects your beliefs.

Can you help us write our vows?

Yes. We can give you prompts, read drafts, or help you shape them from scratch.

Do you do rehearsals?

Yes, for an added fee. A rehearsal helps everyone feel relaxed on the day.

Is our officiant legally able to marry us?

Yes. Chris Joy, our officiant, is an ordained minister, legally able to officiate weddings.

How far will you travel?

Travel is included for the first 30 miles, and beyond that it is charged at the IRS mileage rate. For a few travel weddings each year, we prepare with you online, and you cover flights and lodging, plus $250 for each travel day. We'll marry you in a barn, a courthouse, by a lake, or in your living room.

What if we come from two traditions, or somewhere in-between?

We write ceremonies for interfaith couples, couples from two traditions, and couples who want a fully secular ceremony. For example: "You come from different places and different stories. Today you choose one road, walked side by side."
