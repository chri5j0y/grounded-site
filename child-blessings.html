Child Blessings

Welcoming a new life into a family and a circle of people who will love them.

Plan a Child Blessing

A child blessing gathers the people who will help raise a little one and names the hopes you hold for them. It is for new babies, adopted children, and children joining a blended family.

It can be rooted in your faith, drawn from many traditions, or entirely nonreligious. What matters is that your child is welcomed, named, and surrounded.

What to expect

From first hello to the day itself.

  1. 1

    A first conversation

    Tell us about your child and your family.

  2. 2

    Shaping the blessing

    We talk about the hopes, names, and people you want to include.

  3. 3

    Writing it

    A short, warm ceremony with space for family voices.

  4. 4

    The day

    We lead the blessing at home, in a park, or wherever you gather.

Make it yours

Ways we can shape it

  • The meaning behind your child's name
  • Words from grandparents, godparents, or guideparents
  • Promises from the circle of people who will help raise them
  • Adoption and blended family welcomes
  • A keepsake blessing you can frame
Good to know

Practical details

  • Blessings usually take 15 to 30 minutes.
  • They work well at home, a park, or a family gathering.
  • All faith traditions and everything in-between.
Common questions
Is this a baptism?

It can be part of your faith practice, but it is not a church sacrament. We can complement what your tradition offers.

How old can the child be?

Any age. Some families bless older children joining the family.
