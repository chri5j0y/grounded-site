Hard Seasons

Grief & Caregiver Support

For the ones carrying more than they can say.

Ask About Grief Support

Caregivers hold everyone else together, and grief continues long after the funeral. Both can leave you exhausted in ways the people around you may miss.

These sessions are a place to set it down for an hour, be honest about how it's really going, and find a way forward that lets you rest from being strong all the time.

What it is

What this can look like.

Family Caregivers

Caring for a parent, spouse, or child through illness. Support for the fatigue, the guilt, the grief that starts before the loss, and the hard decisions.

Life After a Loss

For the months and years after someone dies. Grief that keeps its own pace, anniversaries that knock you flat, and learning who you are now.

Care Professionals

Nurses, aides, chaplains, and first responders carrying compassion fatigue or grief still waiting to be felt. Confidential space with someone who has done the work.

Pregnancy and Infant Loss

Support for parents, families, and caregivers through miscarriage, stillbirth, and infant death. Chris is trained through Resolve Through Sharing and has served as a pediatric and NICU chaplain, so he knows how to walk with a family from the first hours to the months after.

Common questions
How long does grief support last?

As long as it helps. Some people come for a few sessions, others for a season.

Can my family come together?

Yes. Family sessions are available at the same rate.

Can my workplace or church help pay?

Often, yes. Many employers and faith communities have wellness or continuing education funds that can cover it.
