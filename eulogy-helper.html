Eulogy Helper

For the one who will stand up and speak about someone they love.

Being asked to speak is an honor, and it can feel like a lot. You don’t need to tell their whole life. A good eulogy says who they were, tells a few true stories, names what they gave you, and says goodbye. Answer the prompts below, and the helper lays your words into that simple shape, with a reading time, so you can make it your own.

Your words stay on this device, private to you. Clear Everything at the bottom removes them.

Who They Were

Three Stories

Small and true is better than big and general. A story that shows who they were does more than a list of good qualities. One honest, human detail helps people recognize them.

What They Gave Us

Speaking Through Tears

Tears are welcome. They mean you loved them, and the room is with you.
