House Blessings

A new home, or a new beginning in an old one.

Plan a House Blessing

A house blessing marks a threshold: moving into a new home, reclaiming a home after a loss, or starting a new chapter under the same roof.

We walk through the home with you and your family, room by room if you like, naming what you hope will happen there: rest, welcome, laughter, and peace.

What to expect

From first hello to the day itself.

  1. 1

    A first conversation

    Tell us about your home and this new season.

  2. 2

    Shaping the blessing

    Choose the rooms, words, and rituals that matter to you.

  3. 3

    The blessing

    We lead a short walk-through blessing with your family and guests.

Make it yours

Ways we can shape it

  • A blessing for each room, or one for the whole home
  • Candles, water, salt, or other traditional elements
  • Words for a fresh start after divorce, loss, or illness
  • Children leading part of the blessing
  • A written blessing to keep by the door
Good to know

Practical details

  • Most blessings take 20 to 40 minutes.
  • Easy to pair with a housewarming gathering.
  • All faith traditions and everything in-between.
Common questions
Is this only for new homes?

Any time is a good time. Many people bless a home after a hard season or a loss.
