The Field Guide is where Grounded’s own team works: Guides, Staff, and Founders, each trained and certified by Grow With Grounded. It holds everything you need to walk with someone through The Grounded Way, in one private workspace, and everything you write stays encrypted on your own device.

What Is Inside

Everything a Guide needs, in one place.

Guided Visits for Every Age

Step by step visits for kids, teens, young adults, grown-ups, older adults, families, and groups, each with a Take-Home Sheet to print.

For Guides Training

A written section and a video for each guide, grouped in rings, with a Certificate of Completion for every ring you finish.

Certified Grounded Guide

Orientation, a signed agreement, the Guide Walkthrough, and a Guide Training with practice scenarios. Finish them and you are certified by Grow With Grounded to use the Grounded tools you trained on.

When Life Changes, for Every Age

Every When Life Changes guide in one place, with words that help for the moments people bring you.

Safety Built In

Consent, the room rule, open questions, and presence in every visit. When harm comes up on its own, you follow the Grounded Response Plan, built on the mandatory reporting law of the state where the visit happens.

Support for Guides

Short practices and a page to read, for your own hard days, plus Learn videos and every Grounded Story in full. Guides tend themselves too.

Your Path

Three clear steps to Certified.

  1. 1

    Begin

    A short orientation, a signed agreement, and the Guide Walkthrough, a video tour of your tools.

  2. 2

    Train

    A Guide Training: short lessons, practice scenarios, and an agreement.

  3. 3

    Certified

    Your Certified Grounded Guide certificate, to print or save, renewed once a year. Each training you pass adds its tool.

Common Questions

Good to know.

Do the people I walk with need the Field Guide?

The Field Guide is for you, the Guide. The people you walk with use The Grounded Way on their own phones, and their answers stay with them.

Where does what I write go?

It stays on your own device, encrypted with your own passcode. You can back it up to a file you keep, so your work stays yours.

What does Certified Grounded Guide mean?

It means you completed Grounded Guide training and are certified by Grow With Grounded to use the Grounded tools listed on your certificate. Each Guide Training you pass adds its tool, and you renew once a year.

Which devices does it run on?

Any phone, tablet, or computer with a current browser. Once opened at home, it keeps working offline.
