For the People Who Walk with Others

The Field Guide

A Guide for every tree, training that goes deep, and a certificate that says you are ready.

Request Access

Grounded’s trees, from Maple to Sequoia, Willow, and The Grove, are open to every person and family on their own phone. The Field Guide is for the people who walk beside them: chaplains, pastors, teachers, coaches, and anyone who guides others through life’s thresholds.

It gathers a Guide for every tree, For Guides training, Learn videos, and every When Life Changes guide in one private workspace. Everything you write stays encrypted on your own device.

We built it from real bedside and classroom work, for all faith traditions and everything in-between.

What It Offers

Everything a Guide needs, in one place.

A Guide for Every Tree

Maple Guide, Aspen Guide, Pine Guide, Birch Guide, Oak Guide, Sequoia Guide, Willow Guide, and Grove Guide for families and groups. Each one walks you through a visit, step by step, with a Take-Home Sheet to print.

For Guides Training

A full written section and a video for each guide, grouped in rings, with a Certificate of Completion for every ring you finish.

Certified Grounded Guide

Orientation, a signed agreement, the Guide Walkthrough, and a Guide Training with practice scenarios. Finish them and you are certified by Grow With Grounded to use the Grounded tools you trained on.

When Life Changes, for Every Age

Every When Life Changes guide from every tree in one place, with words that help for the moments people bring you.

Learn

Short narrated videos, from the Guide Walkthrough to every For Guides series, each with a moment to stop and try it.

Support for Guides

Short practices and a page to read, for your own hard days. Guides tend themselves too.

The Guide’s Shelf

Skills for Every Guide: presence, spiritual care as a skill, and walking with change, drawn from the books on Chris’s shelf, with Quick Cards to print.

Walking with Someone Growing Sober

For every Guide who walks with someone growing sober, thinking about a change, or loving someone who drinks or uses, with Quick Cards to keep close.

Walking with Illness and Disability

A Guide Training for walking with people living with illness or disability, and their families, at every age, as whole people first.

The Hospital Guide

How each unit works, who is there and what they carry, words that help, and a quick card for your pocket.

Safety Built In

Consent, the room rule, and clear steps for hard moments in every visit, set up with your own reporting contact.

Grounded Stories

Real stories from the edge of life, every one open to you in full, to read or to watch.

Your Path

Three clear steps to Certified.

  1. 1

    Begin

    A short orientation, a signed agreement, and the Guide Walkthrough, a video tour of your tools.

  2. 2

    Train

    A Guide Training for the tree you use: short lessons, practice scenarios, and an agreement.

  3. 3

    Certified

    Your Certified Grounded Guide certificate, to print or save, renewed once a year. Each training you pass adds its tool.

Common Questions

Good to know.

Do the people I walk with need the Field Guide too?

The Field Guide is for you, the Guide. The people you walk with use Maple, Aspen, Pine, Birch, Oak, Sequoia, Willow, or The Grove right on their own phones, and their answers stay with them.

Where does what I write go?

It stays on your own device, encrypted with your own passcode. You can back it up to a file you keep, so your work stays yours.

What does Certified Grounded Guide mean?

It means you completed Grounded Guide training and are certified by Grow With Grounded to use the Grounded tools listed on your certificate. Each Guide Training you pass adds its tool, and you renew once a year.

Do I need a faith background?

Grounded welcomes all faith traditions and everything in-between. For Guides teaches spiritual care as a skill: you learn to notice whether faith is a resource for someone and to follow their lead.

How does this fit with counselors, doctors, and therapists?

A Guide offers presence, guidance, and support that walk alongside licensed professionals. Every guide shows when to bring in the right help, and how.

Can my whole team use it?

Yes. Church licenses include 3 Guide seats and school licenses include 5, with more available. See For Organizations

Which devices does it run on?

Any phone, tablet, or computer with a current browser. Once opened at home, it keeps working offline, and it installs on a Mac in its own window.
