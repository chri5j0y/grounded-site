Hard Seasons

Hard Season Support

For the seasons that change everything.

Ask About Hard Season Support

Some seasons break life open: a best friend gone too soon, an overdose, an accident, an addiction, a body that changed overnight, a job or a marriage that ended, a faith that shifted under your feet. Hard Season Support is one-on-one spiritual guidance and emotional support for the person living it, and for the people who love them.

Chris has served as a chaplain in the ICU, in pediatrics and the NICU, and through the COVID pandemic, walking with people through the hardest days of their lives. He stays steady with shock, anger, and grief, and helps you find the next right step.

We welcome all faith traditions and everything in-between, and we always start from where you are.

What it is

What this can look like.

Sudden Loss

When someone is gone without warning: a best friend, a sibling, a coworker, a loss to an overdose or an accident. Room for the shock, the questions, and the grief that comes in waves.

Addiction and Recovery

For the person finding their way to recovery, and for the family and friends who love them. Steady support alongside treatment, meetings, and the people already in your corner.

Life After Injury or Illness

Paralysis, a new diagnosis, a long recovery, or a body that changed overnight. Space to grieve what changed and to find meaning and strength for the life ahead.

Life Changes

A job ending, a move, a breakup or divorce, retirement, or an empty house. Help finding your footing and listening for what comes next.

Faith Changes

Leaving a church, losing religion, or healing from spiritual trauma. Room to be honest about what happened and find your own way forward, wherever it leads.

Common questions
How is this different from therapy?

Therapy treats mental health conditions. Hard Season Support focuses on meaning, faith, values, and finding your footing again. Many people do both, and we're glad to work alongside a therapist.

Can my family come together?

Yes. Family sessions are available at the same rate.

What if faith is part of what hurts?

You're welcome exactly as you are. We follow your lead, and faith comes into the room only when you want it.
