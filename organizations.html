Help your people grow whole.

Membership packs for churches, schools, and workplaces: The Grounded Way for every person, so your people grow healthy in mind, body, spirit, and relationships, every day.

Get a Membership Pack

When your people are healthy in mind, body, spirit, and relationships, your whole community grows stronger. Grounded gives every person a simple way to tend all four, every day.

What a Pack Includes

Full Membership for each of your people.

Full Membership for Each Person

Grounded Nutrition and Fitness, Meditation, Spirit, Relationships with The Grove, the full growth plan, Grounded Seasons, and Grounded Stories. Each person tends four parts, Roots: Spirit, Bark: Mind, Leaves: Body, and Branches: Relationships, and meaning and hope grow from that tending.

A Tree for Every Age

Maple and Aspen for kids, Pine for teens, Birch for young adults, Oak for adults, and Sequoia for older adults, with Living Sober and When Life Changes inside it for a season of life.

Private to Each Person

Everything each person writes stays on their own device, locked with their own passcode. Your organization never sees anyone's answers.

Picture It

One afternoon, then every day.

A church gathers on a Sunday afternoon for a talk on The Grounded Way. Fifty people take Membership home. Each one checks in on their own phone and chooses one practice for each of the four. A few weeks later, a small group is walking together every Wednesday, and the whole church has a shared language for a whole, healthy life.

Grace Fund

Each year, we set aside up to two Membership packs at half the list price, one for a school and one for a church, doing important work on a tight budget. Tell us about yours in a short note.

Who can apply
  • Everyone: Serves mostly people with low incomes.
  • Schools: Public or nonprofit, with at least half of students qualifying for school meal assistance.
  • Churches: A congregation in, or mainly serving, a low-income community.
  • A short reflection at the end of the year: what worked and what could be better. Shared as a group, with every name and answer kept private.

The Grounded Way for your people

The Grounded Way is one private app for whole, healthy, connected living: Today, Check-in, Grow, Learn, and Library. A plan built from each person’s own check-in, food plans from Grounded Recipes, workouts for every level, guided meditations, Morning Quiet, and family nutrition, right beside each person’s tree. Walking with illness and disability

The Grounded Way offers Health and Ability at every age: a private choice, kept locked on the person's own device, that shows the guides, practices, and videos that fit what they live with first. How the tools work for everyone

Disability Ministries

Guides that welcome every member and family, with words for the questions people bring, including the faith questions when they ask them.

Special Education Teams

Maple, Aspen, and Pine guides for a health condition at school, a 504 plan, an IEP, and planning what comes next.

Chronic Illness Support Groups

Pacing, flare days, and rest without guilt, with gentler ways of every practice and a Rest Week whenever it's needed.
