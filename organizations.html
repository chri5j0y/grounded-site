For Organizations

Your program covers every person.

Maple, Aspen, Pine, Birch, Oak, Sequoia, Willow, and The Grove are open to every person and family. The Grounded Field Guide gives your staff what they need to lead them well.

What your license includes

Trained Guides

A Guide for every tree: Maple Guide, Aspen Guide, Pine Guide, Birch Guide, Oak Guide, Sequoia Guide, Willow Guide, and Grove Guide for families and groups. For Guides training comes with scenarios, signed agreements, yearly renewal, and a Certificate of Completion for each person, plus support guides, the Guide’s shelf, and Learn, including Walking with Illness and Disability and Walking with Someone Growing Sober.

Safety Built In

Every visit begins with consent and the room rule, and Guides ask open questions and stay present. When harm comes up on its own, every Guide follows the Grounded Response Plan, built on the mandatory reporting law of the state where the visit happens: the Guide reaches out to the right people and tells the person what they are doing. It is set up with your own crisis lines and reporting contact.

Support for Your People

A launch kit, a person to call, support videos for your own staff, and training days with Chris and Kayti when you want them.

Walking with illness and disability

Every tree from Maple to Sequoia offers Health and Ability: a private choice, kept locked on the person's own device, that shows the guides, practices, and videos that fit what they live with first. Each Guide brings For Guides sections for walking alongside, at every age, and every visit starts with the person's own words and their own yes. How the tools work for everyone

Disability Ministries

Guides that welcome every member and family, with words for the questions people bring, including the faith questions when they ask them.

Special Education Teams

Maple, Aspen, and Pine guides for a health condition at school, a 504 plan, an IEP, and planning what comes next, used with the family's yes.

Pediatric and Rehabilitation Hospitals

Guides for a serious illness or long treatment, life after a brain injury or stroke, and the brothers and sisters at home.

Centers for Independent Living

Tools that start from what a person can do and what the world around them can change, with each person choosing their own words.

Chronic Illness Support Groups

Pacing, flare days, and rest without guilt, with gentler ways of every practice and a Rest Week whenever it's needed.

Parish Nurses

Sequoia Guide and Oak Guide for home visits and congregation visits, with guides for living with several conditions at once.

The Grounded Way for your people

The Grounded Way is one private app for whole, healthy, connected living: Today, Week, Grow, and Library. It is open to everyone your organization serves, and it lives on each person’s own device, locked with their passcode.

Open to Everyone

Food plans for each person’s goals, workouts for every level, Morning Quiet, labs with recommended ranges, and family nutrition, right beside each person’s tree. See What’s Inside

Walked Through by Your Guides

With a license, your Guides learn Tending in For Guides and walk people through it as part of their Guide tools.

A Workshop or Retreat

A half-day workshop or a weekend retreat with Chris and Kayti, for a church, school, or team. Dates and details by request. Ask About a Workshop

Grace Fund

Each year, we set aside up to two licenses at half the list price, one for a school and one for a church, doing important work on a tight budget. Tell us about yours in a short note.

Who can apply
  • Everyone: Serves mostly people with low incomes.
  • Schools: Public or nonprofit, with at least half of students qualifying for free or reduced-price lunch.
  • Churches: A congregation in, or mainly serving, a low-income community.
  • At least one staff member completes Guide training within the first 90 days.
  • A short reflection at the end of the year: what worked and what could be better. Shared as a group, with every name and answer kept private.
  • One license year at a time. Apply again each year. Grace rates and Founding Partner rates are separate offers.
  • Your note: who you serve, why now, and what you're able to pay.
