For couples who would say it all again, and want the people they love to hear it.

Plan Your Vow Renewal

A vow renewal celebrates the marriage you have built: the years, the children, the hard seasons you made it through, and the love that is still here.

It can be a milestone anniversary, a fresh start after a hard season, or simply the celebration you've been waiting for. We help you tell your story and say what the years have taught you.

Common questions
Do we need a license?

Your original license still stands. A vow renewal is a celebration of the marriage you already have.

Can our kids be involved?

Absolutely. Children often make the most moving part of the day.

How far will you travel?

Travel is included for the first 30 miles, and beyond that it is charged at the IRS mileage rate. For a few travel ceremonies each year, we prepare with you online, and you cover flights and lodging, plus $250 for each travel day. We'll lead your renewal in a barn, a courthouse, by a lake, or in your living room.

What if we come from two traditions, or somewhere in-between?

We write ceremonies for interfaith couples, couples from two traditions, and couples who want a fully secular ceremony. For example: "You come from different places and different stories. Today you choose one road, walked side by side."
