Speaking & Training

Real stories and practical tools for the people who do this work.

Ask About Speaking

We speak to hospices, funeral homes, churches, healthcare teams, schools, and community groups about death, grief, and presence. Every talk is built from real bedside stories and ends with something people can use the next day.

We adapt to the room, from a staff in-service of ten nurses to a church basement to a conference keynote, in person or virtual.

What it is

What this can look like.

Spiritual Care at the End of Life

What dying people and families actually need, how to talk about faith and fear, and how to be present when there's nothing to fix. For hospice and palliative teams, clergy, and volunteers.

Grief Debt & Caregiver Burnout

Why helpers carry grief still waiting to be felt, how it shows up, and what to do about it. Drawn from the Grounded essay Grief Debt.

Compassion That Lasts

Practical ways to keep caring deeply while keeping your own cup full. Boundaries, rituals, and recovery for caregivers and care teams.

Whole Person Support with Oak

A hands-on workshop using the Oak check-in to understand and tend spiritual health, for staff or the people they serve.

Grief Groups and Mindfulness Workshops

Support circles, grief groups, and mindfulness workshops for care teams, churches, schools, and community groups, drawn from years of leading daily groups on a behavioral health unit.

Common questions
Can you tailor a talk to our team?

Yes. Every talk starts with a prep call about your audience and what they're facing right now.

Do you speak to small groups?

Yes. From staff in-services of ten people to conferences of hundreds.

What do you need from us?

A room, a screen if you'd like slides, and a sense of who will be there. For events beyond 30 miles of St. Cloud, the host covers travel.
