The Story

One room, one first step.

A room of tired people settles in. They hear a story that sounds like their own life, take one slow breath together, and leave with a plan for the week: a walk outside, a meal at the table, an evening unplugged with the people they love. A month later, they are still doing it.

Common questions

Good to know.

Can you shape a talk for our people?

Yes. Every talk starts with a prep call about your people and what they are carrying right now.

Do you speak to small groups?

Yes. From a staff gathering of ten people to a conference of hundreds.

Is it faith based?

Each talk welcomes all faith traditions and everything in-between. A church may ask for faith woven in, and a school or workplace may ask for plain words. Both are welcome.

What do you need from us?

A room, a screen if you would like slides, and a sense of who will be there.

Ready to bring Grounded to your people?

Tell us a little about your group and your date. We read every message and reply within two days.
