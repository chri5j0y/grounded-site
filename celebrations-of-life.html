Celebrations of Life

Stories, music, laughter, and room for tears.

Plan a Celebration of Life

A celebration of life keeps the focus on how someone lived. It can be relaxed and joyful, held in a backyard, a park, a lake cabin, or a community hall, with stories, music, and food.

Grief still has a place. We make room for both, so people can laugh and cry in the same afternoon and leave feeling closer to the person they miss.

What to expect

From first hello to the day itself.

  1. 1

    A first conversation

    Tell us about the person and the gathering you imagine.

  2. 2

    Gathering stories

    We collect memories from family and friends.

  3. 3

    Shaping the celebration

    A flow with stories, music, open sharing, and a closing moment.

  4. 4

    The gathering

    We host and guide the program so your family can be present.

Make it yours

Ways we can shape it

  • An open mic for stories
  • Their favorite music, food, or place
  • Photo slideshows and memory tables
  • A moment of thanks, a release, or a shared ritual
  • A closing blessing, spiritual or secular
Good to know

Practical details

  • Celebrations can happen weeks or months after a death.
  • Any setting that fits the person.
  • We coordinate with venues and family organizers.
For your family

Tools for Families

Gentle helps for gathering the stories and planning the day, to use on your own or with us. Your words stay on your device.

  • Planning a Farewell: a gentle checklist for the first hours, the first days, the service, and after.
  • Obituary Helper: a Death Notice, a Newspaper Obituary, and an Online Obituary in your own words.
  • Eulogy Helper: prompts, a simple outline, and a reading time for the one who will speak.
Common questions
How is this different from a funeral?

It is usually more relaxed, often later, and focused on celebrating a life. Many families do both.

How soon can you come?

When a death has just happened or is close, mark your message urgent, and we'll usually reach out the same day. We gladly work alongside your funeral home and hospice team.
