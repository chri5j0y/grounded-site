FIRST STEPS AFTER UPLOAD
 1. Open the Field Guide with the Owner code device. Owner tab, Update a Library, Choose Update File,
    pick grove-guide-PATCH-do-not-upload.json. You'll see "Adds groveGuide." Tap Seal It.
 2. A Grove tab appears right away on this device. Try it: add a test person, plant a tree,
    tap a day, open Show me how, choose a different practice, print the weekly sheet.
 3. Owner tab, Ready to upload: download lib-field.js and upload it to the field-guide folder.
    Then delete the patch file from your computer.
 4. When you're ready for Field users to have it: Owner tab, What Field users see, Grove Guide,
    Open to Field. Seal It, download lib-field.js again, upload it.

WHAT'S NEW: GROVE GUIDE (Field Guide, Grove tab)
  Staff and Owner see it as soon as it's sealed. Field users see it after Open to Field, and only
  once Orientation is done. Same rules as Soul Tree Guide.
  One People record per person. A person can have Soul Tree visits and a Grove tree. People added
  in the Grove tab also show in Soul Tree Guide (with "Consent needed" there until asked).
  Consent: The Grove asks for its own yes, on the same screen where you plant. Saying no is
  recorded too, and "Ask Again" is there later.
  Planting: life stage (adult, high school, middle school, kids; kids get kid wording), parts,
  movement level, start date. From a Soul Tree person's page, "Plant Their Grove Tree" comes
  with the parts that weren't strong in their last scored visit already picked.
  Their tree: the same Grove tree picture, growing through the seasons with visitors. Days tended
  (tap the days they report), stay another week, this week's intro, Say and Listen for, the
  week's question with "What they shared," the safety line, every practice with Show me how,
  "Choose a different practice" (their pick stays until changed), private notes (never printed),
  the twelve weeks at a glance, and details you can change.
  Nudges at the end of weeks 5 and 9, and after twelve weeks, with a Soul Tree quick check-in
  or full visit button and "Plant a New Round."
  Printing: This Week's Sheet (for them: practices, busy day versions, days tended, the question
  with room to write, the story, 988 and 911) and What Grew (their tree, parts, and their words).
  Moving trees from the public Grove: "From a Grove File" takes the file from The Grove's
  "Download for the Field Guide" button (or an old Grove backup). Trees saved on this same
  browser before Grounded profiles can move straight across, and it offers to clear them from
  the public Grove afterward. Moving the same file twice doesn't make copies.
  Backups: Grove trees ride along. Days tended and what they shared combine across devices.
  The practices, weeks, and seasons are read from grove/data.js and grove/library.js on the site,
  so editing those files changes both The Grove and the Grove Guide. That needs a connection
  the first time the Grove tab opens in a session.
