Who we are

Grow With Grounded.

Tending the whole person, every age and stage, through every threshold.

We're an independent practice in St. Cloud, Minnesota, serving people anywhere. We lead ceremonies for life's big days, walk with people through hard seasons and the end of life, and build research-informed tools that help every age tend the six parts that make them whole.

It all grows from years at the bedside, where people stop pretending and say what matters.

  • Your story stays yours
  • All faith traditions and everything in-between
  • Grace over pressure
  • Safety first, always
  • Show up

Grow With Grounded is our own independent practice, here to serve anyone, anywhere.
