Privacy

Your life stays yours.

This is how Grow With Grounded and every Grounded tool handle your information, in plain language, on one device or with a Grounded Account. The Grounded Way app and Tending have their own section below, The Grounded Way and Tending, and so do Membership and payments. Last updated November 10, 2026.

Our promise

No ads. No tracking. We never sell, rent, or share anything about you.

What you write in Grounded is locked on your own device before it is saved anywhere, with a key only you hold. That is true on one device, and it stays true with a Grounded Account that brings your tree to every device. We can never read what you write, and no one who breaks into our systems can read it either.

Two ways to use Grounded

On This Device. Grounded works the way it always has: everything you save is locked inside your Grounded profile, in this browser, on this device. Save a Backup and Move to Another Device carry it wherever you want it. Nothing goes to our server.

With a Grounded Account. A Grounded Account is optional. It brings your profiles, your tree, Tending, your check-ins, Family Kitchen, Living Sober, and your Learn marks to every phone, tablet, and computer you sign in on. Everything is locked on your device first, so our server only ever holds locked boxes it can never open.

You can start with either way and change any time. Bring My Profiles to My Account moves what is on this device into your account, and Delete My Account takes it all back out.

What we can see, and what stays yours alone

With a Grounded Account, we can see: - your email address, and whether you have confirmed it - the date your account was made and the last time it signed in - your Membership: the plan, its status, and when the paid time ends - your devices: a short name each device gives itself (like "iPhone, Safari"), when it signed in, and when it last synced - how many locked boxes your account holds, how big they are, and when each one changed - short security records, like the internet address and browser type of each sign in, kept for 30 days

What stays yours alone, locked so only you can open it: - your password and your Root Words (they never leave your device) - the names, pictures, and ages in your profiles, your children's included - your check-ins, answers, scores, growth plan, journal, notes, and saved stories - everything in Tending: food, workouts, labs, heart numbers, weight, and My Plan from My Team - Health and Ability, Living Sober, faith answers, Groundwork, Next Steps, and the Legacy Book - Willow, and everything a helper sees

How the lock works

When you make a Grounded Account, your device makes a random account key, and every box is locked with it before it is saved or sent.

Your password does two jobs, right on your device. It makes a sign in proof, which is all our server receives, and a separate lock key, which never leaves your device and locks your account key. Your 12 Root Words lock the account key a second way, for the day a password slips your mind.

These are the same strong tools Grounded uses today: PBKDF2 with 250,000 rounds, and AES-GCM with 256 bit keys. Our server holds your account key only inside a locked box that opens with your password or your Root Words, and with nothing else.

Root Words and backups

Your passcode stays yours alone, and you hold everything you need to keep your tree safe.

Root Words. When you choose a passcode for a profile (for adults and high schoolers), for a grove in The Grove, or for each partner in Heartwood, you get 12 plain words. A Grounded Account gets its own 12 Root Words too. If you forget your passcode or your password, choose Forgot, then Use My Root Words, and set a new one. Everything you saved stays. Like a seed phrase, just for you.

Root Words are made on your device and never sent anywhere. They are kept only inside your locked profile or account, so you can see them again in Settings after typing your passcode or password. Write them down or print the card, and keep it somewhere private, since your Root Words open your tree the same way your passcode does.

If you forget your account password. Your email shows us it is you, and your Root Words open your account key on your device, so you can set a new password with everything in place. If both the password and the Root Words are lost, your account can start fresh with your email, and the locked boxes are erased, since no one, including us, can open them. We send an email first and wait 24 hours, with a Keep My Account link, so only you can start fresh.

Save a Backup. One locked file holds every profile, grove, and Heartwood on your device, for a lost or broken phone. Save it to your device, iCloud Drive, or another drive. Everything inside stays locked, and opens again with the same passcode or Root Words. Backups work with or without an account.

For kids and middle schoolers, a grown-up who agreed opens the child's profile with their own passcode and sets a new picture code or passcode, so a child is always helped by their grown-up.

In Heartwood, each partner has their own passcode and their own Root Words. A partner who forgets the passcode can use their Root Words, or start fresh with only their own answers cleared. The other partner's answers stay just as they are.

Now and then, after a big moment like a first check-in, a tool may show a gentle note: Save a Backup and keep your Root Words safe. It shows once, and you can close it.

Grounded profiles

A Grounded profile keeps what you save across every Grounded page and tool: your check-ins, your tree, your saved stories, and your name and email for our contact form if you add them.

Everything saved in a profile is encrypted on your device with a key that only your passcode can open (or, for young kids, a picture code). We never see your passcode or anything inside your profile.

With a Grounded Account, the profile's own key is also locked with your account key, so your profiles come along when you sign in on another device. Each profile still opens with its own passcode, so a shared family device stays as private as it is today.

A few things stay open on the device, so a family can see who has a profile on a shared device: each person's name, picture, age group, and, if their "Show my growth on The Grove" switch is on, simple progress, like how many days they've tended their tree and which parts they tended. Anyone can turn that switch off in their tree app. In a Grounded Account, even these open parts are locked inside your account before they reach our server. Answers, journals, notes, levels, and saved stories are always locked. Posts and reactions on The Grove's family wall are shared on purpose with the household on that device, so they are stored there unlocked.

When you create a profile or an account, we record the date you agreed to our Terms of Use and this policy. If either changes, you'll be asked to look it over again. Once you open a profile, it stays open on that device for the time you choose (1, 8, or 24 hours), unless you lock it sooner. Profiles made in Oak before October 2026 move into Grounded profiles the next time they're opened, still locked with the same passcode.

The marks that show which Learn and guide videos you've watched are saved inside your locked profile too. When no profile is open, they are kept only for that visit.

Syncing across your devices

With a Grounded Account, each device sends only what changed, already locked, and brings down what changed elsewhere. When two devices change the same day, both changes are kept: practices, meals, and check-ins add together, settings take the newest change, and something you remove stays removed. Changes made with no connection wait on the device and send when it returns. Grounded keeps working on your device the whole time, connection or none.

What syncs: your profiles and trees (Maple, Aspen, Pine, Birch, Oak, Sequoia, and Willow), The Grounded Way with Tending, Family Kitchen, and Grounded Money, check-ins, Living Sober, Health and Ability, Groundwork, Next Steps, the Legacy Book, and Learn marks.

What stays on the device it was made on: the Field Guide, The Grove, Heartwood, and the page helpers (Obituary Helper, Eulogy Helper, and Planning a Farewell). Each keeps its own passcode and its own backup.

In Settings, My Account shows every device signed in to your account, and Sign Out That Device ends it there. Signing out keeps the locked copy on that device unless you choose Erase This Device Too.

On a shared family computer, each profile still opens only with its own passcode; a signed in account brings a profile’s data down only after that profile opens there.

A Household is up to 6 people, each with their own Grounded Account. The Family Kitchen and Grounded Money can be shared between them, as equals, and everything else stays each person’s own. The person who starts the Household sees who has joined, by email, and never anything anyone writes.

Kids and teens

A grown-up 18 or older makes their own Grounded Account. A kid or teen has an account only on their family's plan, through a parent's or guardian's Household invite, and the parent or guardian approves it first. For a teen (13 to 17), we receive the teen's email, used only for their account. For a kid (12 and under), we receive a sign in name the parent chooses, never a kid's email, and every message about the kid's account goes to the parent. We keep whether an account is a grown-up's, a teen's, or a kid's, so the right Household rules apply. The parent sees each young person's account in the Household, by email or sign in name, never anything they write, and can remove it any time.

A child's Maple or Aspen profile rides inside their grown-up's account, locked with the child's own key, which the grown-up's account holds. A teen's Pine profile rides inside the grown-up's account too, still locked with the teen's own passcode, so only the teen opens it. The grown-up's account carries it and can clear it (erased, never read), the same as on the device today.

We never see a child's name, age, picture, or anything a child enters. Our server holds only locked boxes, and it cannot tell which ones belong to a child.

Maple, Aspen, Pine, and our other tools for young people ask open questions about growth, and a child's answers stay locked. A profile for anyone under 18 needs a grown-up to agree first.

For kids and middle schoolers, the grown-ups who agree can open the child's profile with their own passcode, so kids are never alone with something hard. Kids are told this plainly before they start. For high schoolers in Pine, answers and journal entries are locked with the teen's own passcode, and no one else can open them. Grown-ups can see a teen's progress in The Grove (the parts they're tending, days tended, and visitors), and the teen's answers stay with the teen. Teens are told this before they start. If a teen forgets their passcode, their own Root Words open it again. Without them, a grown-up can clear the teen's private entries (erased, never read) so they can start fresh.

Every check-in screen shows a quiet line about who to talk to and how to reach help, the same for every child.

The Grounded Way and Tending

The Grounded Way is our app at growwithgrounded.com/home/, with Today, Check-in, Grow, Learn, and Library in one place. It lives inside your Grounded profile, the same way every tree does, locked with your passcode. Along with your tree, it keeps My Growth Plan (the choices you make for Body, Mind, Spirit, Table, and practices, and the rhythm of each practice), and the days you tended it.

What Tending keeps. Tending is the healthy living part of The Grounded Way. It keeps what you choose to enter: your goals, your food needs, and any allergies you type; your household (how many grown-ups, and each child's first name, age range, and sex, for portions and daily amounts); your food plan, My Menu, the holidays you plan, My Own Items, and shopping lists; the meals and foods you log; your workouts, your level, and the weights you lift; your daily checks and Morning Quiet; your start-up check answers; your weight and other markers, if you add them; your lab numbers and blood pressure; your LDL goal and your LDL Path choices; the heart risk scores you add from your doctor and the numbers you type into the PREVENT calculator; your notes; and My Plan from My Team, kept just as you wrote it.

Where it lives. All of it is saved inside your Grounded profile, encrypted with the same key your passcode and Root Words open. With a Grounded Account, it syncs to your other devices in locked boxes. Save a Backup carries it inside your one locked file too. Grow With Grounded can never read it: no food logs, no health numbers, nothing you type. It reaches another person only when you choose to share something yourself, like copying a shopping list into a text.

Recorded voices. In Tending, recorded voices coach the workouts and practices and guide the kitchen. Each recording is an audio file your browser fetches from our audio host, audio.growwithgrounded.com, served by Cloudflare. Every file is named from a line Grounded wrote, never from anything you enter, so each request carries only what a normal web request carries, like your internet address and browser type, and no personal or health information. With Voice Off, Tending fetches no recordings. The soft music under a session plays from our own website.

Kids and teens. For kids in Maple and Aspen, The Grounded Way shows Today and My Tree, set up with their grown-up. Family nutrition is logged by a grown-up, inside the grown-up's own Tending, and it tracks the food, never the body: food groups and nutrients, with no weight and no calories for anyone under 18. A teen who uses Tending in their own profile sees no weight and no calories either, and only the teen's passcode opens it. In Your Family, a grown-up sees each child's progress only, like days tended this week, their season, and their rings.

Starting fresh. Entries like notes, lab panels, heart risk scores, and My Plan from My Team each have their own Remove button. To erase a profile, open Manage My Profile (tap your initials at the top of The Grounded Way, then Privacy) and choose to remove it. With a Grounded Account, removing a profile removes it from every device and from our server. Save a Backup first to keep anything.

Locked with a passcode

The Grounded Field Guide locks its records with a passcode you choose, using the same strong encryption on your device. We never see your passcode, so a forgotten one is opened only by your own backup and Root Words. Keep a backup file.

Faith or Plain wording

Our tools include spiritual content: questions, practices, and guides about faith, meaning, and hope, written for all faith traditions and everything in-between. In the kids' and teen trees (Maple, Aspen, and Pine), the grown-up who sets up the tree chooses the wording. Faith wording comes first, and names God, prayer, and faith as one door among several. Plain wording asks the same things without religious words, and it is there for any family or school that wants it. Birch offers the same choice to the young adult. Scores compare the same either way, and the choice is saved inside the locked profile, so it stays yours alone.

Health and Ability

Health and Ability is a setting in Maple, Aspen, Pine, Birch, Oak, and Sequoia, and in Manage My Profile. A person can choose anything that's part of their life right now, like a long-term health condition, chronic pain, moving differently, being Deaf or hard of hearing, being blind or having low vision, or someone close to them living with one. They can also choose None Right Now or I'd Rather Not Say. It is always optional, and it's offered once, quietly, after a first full check-in, and then waits in Settings.

The choice changes only what shows first: guides, practices, and videos that fit, a short example under some check-in questions, and a few information and support lines below the crisis lines. The questions, the scores, and every help line stay exactly the same.

The choice is saved once, inside the person's locked profile, and every tree reads it from there. With a Grounded Account it syncs locked, like everything else in the profile, and Grow With Grounded can never read it. It is kept out of the open parts of a profile, out of The Grove, out of Visit cards, Share to Family, Willow cards, and printed plans. A guest's choice lasts only for that visit. Living Sober is one of these choices, and it is kept exactly the same way, along with its one follow-up question, inside the locked profile.

For kids and middle schoolers, the grown-up who can open the child's profile sets it with the child, and the child's own view shows only the guides and practices that fit, never a label. In Pine, only the teen can set it and only the teen's passcode opens it. In Birch and Sequoia, it stays private from helpers unless the person turns on "Share My Health and Ability with my helpers." A Guide using the Field Guide never asks anyone to choose.

Clear My Choices, in the same setting, erases the choice completely, on every device. Moving to the next tree keeps it with the profile, and starting a new Birch profile asks first whether to bring it along.

Show Gentler Ways First and Rest Week are open to everyone, with no health choice needed. They are saved in the locked profile in the same way.

Willow, and helpers

Willow is built for a person in hospice and the people who love them. The person's Willow lives in their own Grounded profile, locked with their passcode: their check-ins, their faith answers, what matters to them, their Cuttings, and the log of what helped. Grow With Grounded can never read it, and only the people the person chooses can open it.

A family member or friend can be a helper. A helper has their own profile and passcode, and the person's profile can be opened with the helper's passcode, the same way a grown-up opens a child's profile. Helpers are added with their own passcode typed in, and can be removed in Willow's settings at any time.

The person chooses what helpers see in Willow: how their tree is doing (in words, never answers), what matters to them, their Cuttings, the log, their faith answers, and notes from check-ins. Faith answers and notes start private. Please know that this choice is kept by Willow itself, on a shared device, so anyone who can open the profile could in principle see what is saved in it. Only add helpers you trust.

Every check-in records who answered: the person, the person with a helper tapping, or a helper answering from what they see. A helper's observations are kept apart and never speak for the person's own tree. After a death, a helper can mark the tree as remembered. Nothing is erased.

Willow can share with your hospice chaplain or doula, in person, and only when you choose. You pick what goes: check-ins, faith answers, what matters, and notes, and only what the person already chose to share. A chaplain or doula can send a card back from a visit, with a practice to try, what matters, and the vigil plan. Either way, the card is sealed with a short code you read out loud, and it travels inside a QR code or link after the #, the part a browser never sends to any server. It never passes through us. Once a chaplain or doula adds it, it's kept in their own encrypted Field Guide, under their organization's privacy rules.

Pine, for high schoolers

Pine is built for high schoolers, grades 9 to 12. A teen's Pine lives in their own Grounded profile, and only the teen's own passcode opens it: their check-ins, their growth plan and practices, their journal, and Next Steps, their notebook for plans after high school. Grow With Grounded can never read it, and Pine sends no alerts to anyone.

The grown-up who agreed when the profile was made sees only the simple progress described above, if the teen's "Show my growth on The Grove" switch is on. Answers, levels, notes, faith answers, and Next Steps stay with the teen. Pine's optional questions start off, and only the teen can turn them on.

Birch, for young adults

Birch is built for young adults, 18 to 26. A person's Birch lives in their own Grounded profile, and only their own passcode opens it: their check-ins, their growth plan and practices, their tree's levels and milestones, and Groundwork, their private notebook for plans and skills. Grow With Grounded can never read any of it. Groundwork reaches another person only when the person chooses to print or save it.

Helpers are off until the person turns them on in Birch's settings. A helper, like a parent, a mentor, or a friend, has their own profile and passcode, and sees only what the person chooses to share. Groundwork stays with the person and is never shared to family.

Sequoia, and helpers

Sequoia is built for older adults. The person's Sequoia lives in their own Grounded profile, locked with their passcode: their check-ins, their growth plan and practices, and their Legacy Book. Grow With Grounded can never read it. A page of the Legacy Book reaches another person only when the person chooses to print or share it.

Helpers are off until the person turns them on in Sequoia's settings. A helper, like an adult child, a spouse, or a friend, has their own profile and passcode, and sees only what the person chooses to share. Faith answers and notes start private. Check-ins a helper takes with the person record who answered, and a helper answering from what they see is kept apart from the person's own tree.

Helpers also have a check-in of their own, with questions for the person doing the helping. Their answers stay in the helper's own profile, locked with the helper's passcode.

Safety

Grounded tools are for reflection and growth. Our self-guided tools ask open, curious questions about growth and ask nothing about harm, so they save nothing about harm and send no alerts. Your answers stay locked, and no one at Grounded sees them, on one device or in a Grounded Account. Answers saved by an earlier version of a tool stay locked, untouched, and are no longer shown or used.

If you ever feel like hurting yourself, call or text 988, any time. In Minnesota you can also call **CRISIS (274747). In danger now, call 911.

Guide-led work and the Grounded Response Plan

When you meet with a Grounded Guide, Staff member, or Founder, they ask open questions and listen with presence, staying curious and without judgement. Sometimes harm comes up on its own in a conversation. When it does, they follow the Grounded Response Plan.

Grounded follows the mandatory reporting law of the state where a visit happens, with Minnesota law as our baseline. When a guide believes a person may harm themselves or someone else, or that a child or vulnerable adult may be harmed, the guide reaches out to the right people: 988, 911, the county crisis team or child protection, or the Minnesota Adult Abuse Reporting Center (MAARC). The guide tells the person what they are doing and why. The guide also lets Grounded's founders know the same day, by phone, and keeps a short factual note of what happened and who was contacted in their own locked records.

Delete My Account

Settings, My Account, Delete My Account. You type DELETE and your password to confirm, and then: - every locked box, your device list, your sign in, and your account record are erased from our live systems at once - the short service backups our host keeps roll off within 30 days, and they stay locked the whole time - your Membership through Stripe ends at once, and no further payment is taken - this device keeps its own copy, still locked, so you can keep using Grounded On This Device; choose Erase This Device Too to remove it here as well - we send one email to say it is done

Stripe keeps its own payment records, as payment law requires (see Membership and Payments).

Your choices and your rights

You can use every tool with no profile and no account, and give us nothing at all.

With a Grounded Account you can: - see what we hold: Download My Account Data gives you your email, Membership, devices, and dates, plus every locked box, which opens on your own device - change your email: in My Account, confirmed by a link to the new address - delete everything: Delete My Account, above - ask us anything: write to hello@growwithgrounded.com, and we answer within 45 days. If you disagree with our answer, write back and ask us to look again, and we will tell you what we decided and why.

We never sell your information, and we never use it for ads.

Membership and payments

Grounded Membership is paid through Stripe, a payment service. You enter your card on Stripe’s own page, and Stripe keeps it under its own privacy policy. We never see or keep your card number.

From Stripe, we receive what the payment needs: your email, the plan you chose, and your payment status, like whether your membership is active, renewed, or ended. We use your email to send your Member code (a Household gets one code for each person), to answer your questions, and to help with a payment or a refund. We never add you to a list unless you ask.

Your Member code opens Membership on the device where you enter it, and the key stays there, in your browser. Membership also opens Grounded Stories in full on that device. Everything else stays locked, on one device or in a Grounded Account: we never see your check-ins, your plan, your Tending, or which practices you open.

An organization with a Membership pack receives codes from us to give its people, and it never sees anyone’s answers. A Supporter may receive a personal code for Grounded Stories from us.

Email we send

With a Grounded Account, we send only the emails an account needs: - a link to confirm your email - a link to reset your password - a short note when your account signs in on a new device - a note when your account is deleted, or when Start Fresh is asked for - important notices about your account, this policy, or your security

We send no newsletters and no offers from your account. Our emails are sent through Postmark, with open and click tracking turned off; Postmark keeps a short log of each email (the address, the subject, and when it was sent) to deliver it.

If something ever goes wrong

We guard what we can see (your email, your Membership, and your device list) with close attention: strong locks, few people with access, and a steady watch on our systems.

If anyone ever gets into our systems, we will tell you by email as soon as we can, and within 60 days at most, along with what was involved and what we are doing. We will also tell the authorities the law names, like the Federal Trade Commission. Because what you write is locked on your device before it leaves, a break-in could reach only what we can see, never what you write.

When you contact us

If you write to us through our contact form or at hello@growwithgrounded.com, we receive what you send, like your name, email, and message, and use it only to reply and to help you. We don't add you to any list unless you ask. Our contact form is delivered by a service called Formspree, which passes your message to our email.

Outside services

Our website is hosted by GitHub Pages and protected by Cloudflare. Like almost every website host, they may briefly keep basic technical records, such as your internet address, to deliver pages and stop attacks. We don't use those records to track you.

Grounded Accounts run on Cloudflare (Workers, D1, and R2), at api.growwithgrounded.com, with data kept in North America. Cloudflare holds only what this page says we can see, plus the locked boxes. Recorded voices in Tending play from our audio host, audio.growwithgrounded.com, also served by Cloudflare. Account emails are sent by Postmark. Payments are handled by Stripe. We host our own fonts, so your visit isn't shared with any font service. Links to other websites, like helplines or Substack, follow those sites' own policies.

The library and Amazon links

The Book Library is a list of books, kept on our own site. The "Find on Amazon" links on each book open Amazon in your browser. Nothing on our site tracks you or tells Amazon who you are. Once you land on Amazon, Amazon's own privacy policy applies. If a link carries our Amazon Associates tag, Grow With Grounded may earn a small amount from qualifying purchases. It never changes which books are in the library, and the library page says so whenever a tag is in use.

Guides, Staff, and Founders

The Grounded Field Guide is for Grounded’s own team of Guides, Staff, and Founders, who use it with the people they walk with. Their records stay encrypted on their own devices, not with us. They follow the Grounded Response Plan and the law, including health privacy laws like HIPAA where they apply.

Changes

If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page and the date at the top. With a Grounded Account, we also email you before a change that matters goes live, and you'll be asked to look it over the next time you open Grounded.
