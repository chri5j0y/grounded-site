Privacy

Your life stays yours.

This is how Grow With Grounded and every Grounded tool handle your information, in plain language. Last updated October 10, 2026.

Our promise

No accounts with us. No ads. No tracking. We never sell, rent, or share anything about you. Our tools don't send your answers to us or to anyone else.

Where your answers live

Maple, Aspen, Pine, Birch, Oak, Sequoia, Willow, The Grove, and the Field Guide run entirely in your web browser. Everything you enter, like check-ins, practices, journal notes, and profile pictures, is saved only in that browser, on that device. We can't see it, and we couldn't recover it if it were lost.

That also means your information stays with you on that device. To move it or keep a copy, use Save a Backup (Back up everything in a tool's Settings) to save one locked file. Keep that file somewhere private, like your device, iCloud Drive, or another drive.

To erase everything, use the delete options inside each tool, or clear this website's data in your browser settings.

Grounded profiles

A Grounded profile keeps what you save across every Grounded page and tool: your check-ins, your tree, your saved stories, and your name and email for our contact form if you add them. It is not an account with us. It lives only in this browser, on this device, and it doesn't follow you to another phone or computer.

Everything saved in a profile is encrypted on your device with a key that only your passcode can open (or, for young kids, a picture code). We never see your passcode or anything inside your profile. That is what keeps it private, and it is why your Root Words and backups, described below, are how you open it again yourself.

A few things are not locked, so a family can see who has a profile on a shared device: each person's name, picture, age group, and, if their "Show my growth on The Grove" switch is on, simple progress, like how many days they've tended their tree and which parts they tended. Anyone can turn that switch off in their tree app. Answers, journals, notes, levels, and saved stories are always locked. Posts and reactions on The Grove's family wall are shared on purpose with the household on that device, so they are stored there unlocked.

When you create a profile, we record on your device the date you agreed to our Terms of Use and this policy. If either changes, you'll be asked to look it over again. Once you open a profile, it stays open on that device for the time you choose (1, 8, or 24 hours), unless you lock it sooner. Profiles made in Oak before October 2026 move into Grounded profiles the next time they're opened, still locked with the same passcode.

The marks that show which Learn and guide videos you've watched are saved inside your locked profile too. When no profile is open, they are kept only for that visit. Marks saved by an earlier version of a tool move into your profile the next time you open it.

Root Words and backups

Your passcode stays yours alone, and you hold everything you need to keep your tree safe.

Root Words. When you choose a passcode for a profile (for adults and high schoolers), for a grove in The Grove, or for each partner in Heartwood, you get 12 plain words. If you forget your passcode, choose Forgot your passcode, then Use My Root Words, and set a new one. Everything you saved stays. Like a seed phrase, just for you.

Root Words are made on your device and never sent anywhere. They are kept only inside your locked profile, so you can see them again in Settings after typing your passcode. Write them down or print the card, and keep it somewhere private, since your Root Words open your tree the same way your passcode does.

Save a Backup. One locked file holds every profile, grove, and Heartwood on your device, for a lost or broken phone. Save it to your device, iCloud Drive, or another drive. Everything inside stays locked, and opens again with the same passcode or Root Words.

For kids and middle schoolers, a grown-up who agreed opens the child's profile with their own passcode and sets a new picture code or passcode, so a child is always helped by their grown-up.

In Heartwood, each partner has their own passcode and their own Root Words. A partner who forgets the passcode can use their Root Words, or start fresh with only their own answers cleared. The other partner's answers stay just as they are.

Now and then, after a big moment like a first check-in, a tool may show a gentle note: Save a backup and keep your Root Words safe. It shows once, and you can close it.

Locked with a passcode

The Grounded Field Guide locks its records with a passcode you choose, using the same strong encryption on your device. We never see your passcode, so if you forget it, we can't open your records for you. Keep a backup file.

Kids and teens

Maple, Aspen, Pine, and our other tools for young people collect nothing, and nothing is sent anywhere. A child's answers stay on the family's or school's device. A profile for anyone under 18 needs a grown-up to agree first.

For kids and middle schoolers, the grown-ups who agree can open the child's profile with their own passcode, so kids are never alone with something hard. Kids are told this plainly before they start. For high schoolers in Pine, answers and journal entries are locked with the teen's own passcode, and no one else can open them. Grown-ups can see a teen's progress in The Grove (the parts they're tending, days tended, and visitors) and are alerted if a safety answer needs attention, but never the answer itself. Teens are told this before they start. If a teen forgets their passcode, their own Root Words open it again. Without them, a grown-up can clear the teen's private entries (erased, never read) so they can start fresh.

If a child's answers suggest they may be struggling, the tool gently shows who to talk to and how to reach help.

Faith or Plain wording

Our tools include spiritual content: questions, practices, and guides about faith, meaning, and hope, written for all faith traditions and everything in-between. In the kids' and teen trees (Maple, Aspen, and Pine), the grown-up who sets up the tree chooses the wording. Faith wording comes first, and names God, prayer, and faith as one door among several. Plain wording asks the same things without religious words, and it is there for any family or school that wants it. Birch offers the same choice to the young adult. Scores compare the same either way, and the choice is saved on the device with the rest of the profile, so nothing about it is sent to us.

Health and Ability

Health and Ability is a setting in Maple, Aspen, Pine, Birch, Oak, and Sequoia, and in Manage My Profile. A person can choose anything that's part of their life right now, like a long-term health condition, chronic pain, moving differently, being Deaf or hard of hearing, being blind or having low vision, or someone close to them living with one. They can also choose None Right Now or I'd Rather Not Say. It is always optional, and it's offered once, quietly, after a first full check-in, and then waits in Settings.

The choice changes only what shows first: guides, practices, and videos that fit, a short example under some check-in questions, and a few information and support lines below the crisis lines. The questions, the scores, and every safety question, alert, and help line stay exactly the same.

The choice is saved once, inside the person's locked profile, and every tree reads it from there. It stays on the device and is never sent to us or anyone else. Grow With Grounded receives no health information at all. It is kept out of the open parts of a profile, out of The Grove, out of Visit cards, Share to Family, Willow cards, and printed plans. A guest's choice lasts only for that visit.

For kids and middle schoolers, the grown-up who can open the child's profile sets it with the child, and the child's own view shows only the guides and practices that fit, never a label. In Pine, only the teen can set it and only the teen's passcode opens it; Pine's grown-up alerts never mention it. In Birch and Sequoia, it stays private from helpers unless the person turns on "Share My Health and Ability with my helpers." A Guide using the Field Guide never asks anyone to choose.

Clear My Choices, in the same setting, erases the choice completely. Moving to the next tree keeps it with the profile, and starting a new Birch profile asks first whether to bring it along.

Show Gentler Ways First and Rest Week are open to everyone, with no health choice needed. They are saved in the locked profile in the same way.

Willow, and helpers

Willow is built for a person in hospice and the people who love them. The person's Willow lives in their own Grounded profile, locked with their passcode: their check-ins, their faith answers, what matters to them, their Cuttings, and the log of what helped. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

A family member or friend can be a helper. A helper has their own profile and passcode, and the person's profile can be opened with the helper's passcode, the same way a grown-up opens a child's profile. Helpers are added with their own passcode typed in, and can be removed in Willow's settings at any time.

The person chooses what helpers see in Willow: how their tree is doing (in words, never answers), what matters to them, their Cuttings, the log, their faith answers, and notes from check-ins. Faith answers and notes start private. An answer about feeling safe at home is never shown to helpers. Please know that this choice is kept by Willow itself, on a shared device, so anyone who can open the profile could in principle see what is saved in it. Only add helpers you trust.

Every check-in records who answered: the person, the person with a helper tapping, or a helper answering from what they see. A helper's observations are kept apart and never speak for the person's own tree. After a death, a helper can mark the tree as remembered. Nothing is erased.

Willow can share with your hospice chaplain or doula, in person, and only when you choose. You pick what goes: check-ins, faith answers, what matters, and notes, and only what the person already chose to share. A chaplain or doula can send a card back from a visit, with a practice to try, what matters, and the vigil plan. Either way, the card is sealed with a short code you read out loud, and it travels inside a QR code or link after the #, the part a browser never sends to any server. It never passes through us. The answer about feeling safe at home is never shared. Once a chaplain or doula adds it, it's kept in their own encrypted Field Guide, under their organization's privacy rules.

Pine, for high schoolers

Pine is built for high schoolers, grades 9 to 12. A teen's Pine lives in their own Grounded profile, and only the teen's own passcode opens it: their check-ins, their growth plan and practices, their journal, and Next Steps, their notebook for plans after high school. All of it stays on the device, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

The grown-up who agreed when the profile was made sees only a quiet "Please check in" alert in The Grove when a check-in asks for a caring conversation, plus the simple progress described above if the teen's "Show my growth on The Grove" switch is on. Never answers, levels, notes, faith answers, or Next Steps. When a teen says someone is hurting them, that answer never goes to the family alert; Pine shows the teen people outside the home who can help. Pine's optional questions start off, and only the teen can turn them on.

Birch, for young adults

Birch is built for young adults, 18 to 26. A person's Birch lives in their own Grounded profile, and only their own passcode opens it: their check-ins, their growth plan and practices, their tree's levels and milestones, and Groundwork, their private notebook for plans and skills. All of it stays on the device, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else. Birch sends no alert to anyone. Groundwork stays on the device too, and leaves it only when the person chooses to print or save it.

Helpers are off until the person turns them on in Birch's settings. A helper, like a parent, a mentor, or a friend, has their own profile and passcode, and sees only what the person chooses to share. Safety answers are never shown to a helper, and Groundwork is never shared to family.

Sequoia, and helpers

Sequoia is built for older adults. The person's Sequoia lives in their own Grounded profile, locked with their passcode: their check-ins, their growth plan and practices, and their Legacy Book. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else. The Legacy Book stays on the device too, and a page leaves it only when the person chooses to print or share it.

Helpers are off until the person turns them on in Sequoia's settings. A helper, like an adult child, a spouse, or a friend, has their own profile and passcode, and sees only what the person chooses to share. Faith answers and notes start private, and safety answers are never shown to a helper. Check-ins a helper takes with the person record who answered, and a helper answering from what they see is kept apart from the person's own tree.

Helpers also have a check-in of their own, with questions for the person doing the helping. Their answers stay in the helper's own profile, locked with the helper's passcode.

Safety

Grounded tools are for reflection and growth. They are not a screening, a diagnosis, therapy, or medical care. If you or someone you love is in crisis, call or text 988, any time. In Minnesota you can also call **CRISIS (274747) or text MN to 741741. If someone is in immediate danger, call 911.

When you contact us

If you write to us through our contact form or at hello@growwithgrounded.com, we receive what you send, like your name, email, and message, and use it only to reply and to help you. We don't add you to any list unless you ask. Our contact form is delivered by a service called Formspree, which passes your message to our email.

If you subscribe to our stories, that happens on Substack, which has its own privacy policy.

Outside services

Our website is hosted by GitHub Pages and protected by Cloudflare. Like almost every website host, they may briefly keep basic technical records, such as your internet address, to deliver pages and stop attacks. We don't use those records to track you. We host our own fonts, so your visit isn't shared with any font service. Links to other websites, like helplines or Substack, follow those sites' own policies.

The library and Amazon links

The Grounded library is a list of books, kept on our own site. The "Find on Amazon" links on each book open Amazon in your browser. Nothing on our site tracks you or tells Amazon who you are. Once you land on Amazon, Amazon's own privacy policy applies. If a link carries our Amazon Associates tag, Grow With Grounded may earn a small amount from qualifying purchases. It never changes which books are in the library, and the library page says so whenever a tag is in use.

Practitioners

Chaplains, pastors, teachers, counselors, and others use the Grounded Field Guide with the people they serve. Their records stay encrypted on their own devices, not with us. They're responsible for following their own organization's privacy rules and the law, including health privacy laws like HIPAA where they apply.

Your choices, and changes

To ask for help using our site or tools in a way that works for you, see our Accessibility page.

You can use every tool without a profile and without giving us anything. You can delete a profile from its own settings at any time. If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
