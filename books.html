Why Read It

What if the room where a life ends could teach us how to live?

Chris Joy has spent years at the bedside, in farmhouses and memory care, ICU rooms and small apartments. He came to offer presence. He left with lessons. Again and again, the people there wanted one gift: permission to grieve. To say what matters. To pray their own way. To let go.

About the Author

Written from years at the bedside.

Love and loss come to every family. This book meets you there, one gentle story at a time, so you can face your own seasons with more courage and more love.

Chris Joy is a chaplain and the co-founder of Grow With Grounded. He has served at the bedside in hospice, in the ICU, and on a behavioral health unit. He lives in St. Cloud, Minnesota, with his family.

In the End is the first book from Grow With Grounded, where the Grounded team walks with people and families through every season of life.

Read the Opening

Grounded in Coffee

The other day I was having one of those rough starts we all know too well. Everything already felt a little heavier than usual.

I was heading out on the highway to my first visit when a reckless driver came flying past and cut right in front of me. I slammed on the brakes. My work bag flew forward and knocked my full coffee cup across the seat. The outside of the car was fine. The inside? Total disaster, coffee everywhere.

Of course, the same driver ended up stopped at the red light just ahead of me. I pulled up beside him and gave him my best stink eye. He rolled down his window and started yelling at me like it was my fault.

In that moment, I had to remind myself: You are a hospice chaplain on your way to care for patients and families. You cannot do this. I kept my eyes forward, left the window up, and let him speed away. But my whole body was shaking with adrenaline.

I had only a few minutes before my first visit, and I needed to show up calm, present, and actually useful to the family I was about to meet. So I pulled over near a quiet field, took a deep breath, and used the 5-4-3-2-1 grounding technique right there in the driver’s seat.

The story continues in the book.
