Questions about Gift Membership

When do their 12 months begin?

The day they enter the code. A gift code waits until they are ready, so a birthday, a graduation, or a quiet Monday all work well.

How do I give the code?

Your gift code arrives by email, sent to the email you pay with. Write it in a card, read it aloud, or send it in a message. The gift note above helps you say it simply.

What do they need to begin?

They make their own Grounded Account with their email, then enter the code once under Membership, I Have a Code. Membership then opens on every device they sign in on.

Can I give more than one?

Yes. Give one gift for each person, and each gift has its own code. For everyone in one home, a Household Gift covers up to six people with one code.

How does a Household Gift work?

One grown-up in the home makes a Grounded Account and enters the code once under Membership, I Have a Code. They add the family under Household, and everyone in the home, up to six people, has full Membership for 12 months.

What happens after 12 months?

Their Membership time ends, and everything they made stays right where it is. Start stays open to them every day, and they can join as a Member any time to keep the full suite.

Is what they write private?

Yes. Everything they write stays locked with their own passcode. The gift opens Membership for them, and their answers belong to them alone.
