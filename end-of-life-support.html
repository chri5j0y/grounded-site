End-of-Life Support

Presence for the last chapter, for the person dying and the people who love them.

Ask About End-of-Life Support

Hospice teams do sacred work with heavy caseloads. We can stay. We offer unhurried, non-medical presence for people who are dying and for their families: time to talk honestly, plan what matters, sit through the long nights, and make sure someone is beside your person at the end.

Chris has served as a chaplain in hospice, the ICU, and through the COVID pandemic, and Kayti spent seven years caring for her own family at the end of life. We know what the last days can look like, and how to help a family through them.

We also walk with families in pediatrics and the NICU, where Chris has served as a chaplain, with the same unhurried presence.

What it is

What this can look like.

Vigil Sitting

In the final days and hours, we sit with your person so family can rest, eat, or step away with peace of mind. Quiet presence, prayer if it's wanted, and gentle updates on what's happening.

End-of-Life Planning

Who's in the room? What music is playing? What should be said, and what should be left alone? We put their wishes on paper so everyone knows what to do when the time comes. This plans the experience, alongside any legal or medical documents.

Family Support

Explaining what dying looks like so it's less frightening, helping family members talk with each other, and guiding the hard conversations about saying goodbye.

Life Review & Legacy

Guided conversations about a life well lived, recorded stories, letters to grandchildren, or a simple legacy project the family can keep.

Common questions
Is this instead of hospice?

No. We work alongside hospice and medical teams. We don't provide medical care, and we'll always encourage you to use hospice if you qualify.

What if we're not religious?

That's completely fine. We follow your lead. Prayer and faith are offered when you want them.
